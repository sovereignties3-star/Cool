EO-SEC-17-001 # EXECUTIVE ORDER: SEC ENCROACHMENT FIREWALL AND DOMESTIC CAPITAL PRESERVATION
EO-SEC-17-002 
EO-SEC-17-003 ## PART 33: ADMINISTRATIVE JURISDICTIONAL FIREWALL (SEC LIMITATION)
EO-SEC-17-004 
EO-SEC-17-005 ### Section 33.1: Declaration of Policy and Jurisdictional Boundaries
EO-SEC-17-006 This Section establishes a formal administrative firewall restricting the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)
EO-SEC-17-007 from expanding its regulatory jurisdiction beyond the explicit statutory boundaries enacted by Congress.
EO-SEC-17-008 In accordance with recent Supreme Court precedents limiting administrative agency overreach, including the
EO-SEC-17-009 Major Questions Doctrine (*West Virginia v. EPA*) and the dissolution of Chevron deference (*Loper Bright
EO-SEC-17-010 Enterprises v. Raimondo*), the SEC shall not unilaterally extend the definition of an "investment contract"
EO-SEC-17-011 under the Securities Act of 1933 (48 Stat. 74, 15 U.S.C. § 77a et seq.) to decentralized protocols,
EO-SEC-17-012 software-defined assets, or self-executing smart contracts lacking centralized managerial efforts.
EO-SEC-17-013 
EO-SEC-17-014 ### Section 33.2: Inter-Agency Coordination and Conflict Resolution
EO-SEC-17-015 To prevent regulatory duplication and jurisdictional encroachment, a Joint Regulatory Council is hereby
EO-SEC-17-016 established, comprising representatives from the SEC, the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC),
EO-SEC-17-017 the Federal Trade Commission (FTC), and state-level financial regulatory authorities.
EO-SEC-17-018 1. The SEC is prohibited from initiating enforcement actions against entities operating within the
EO-SEC-17-019    jurisdictional purview of the CFTC or state regulators without a prior 90-day consultation period.
EO-SEC-17-020 2. Any determination of asset classification (security vs. commodity) must be agreed upon by a majority
EO-SEC-17-021    vote of the Joint Regulatory Council, preventing unilateral SEC enforcement-led rulemaking.
EO-SEC-17-022 3. State-level consumer protection and financial regulations shall be respected as primary authorities
EO-SEC-17-023    unless directly preempted by explicit federal statutory provisions.
EO-SEC-17-024 
EO-SEC-17-025 ### Section 33.3: Protection of Software Developers and Protocol Validators
EO-SEC-17-026 No software developer, protocol validator, node operator, or non-custodial wallet provider shall be
EO-SEC-17-027 classified as a "broker," "dealer," "clearing agency," or "exchange" under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934
EO-SEC-17-028 solely for writing, publishing, or running open-source code. The SEC's jurisdiction is strictly limited
EO-SEC-17-029 to intermediaries exercising direct, discretionary custody of third-party investor funds.
EO-SEC-17-030 
EO-SEC-17-031 ## PART 34: DOMESTIC CAPITAL PRESERVATION MANDATE
EO-SEC-17-032 
EO-SEC-17-033 ### Section 34.1: Security of Domestic Capital Flows
EO-SEC-17-034 To safeguard the economic sovereignty of the United States, this Mandate establishes strict controls
EO-SEC-17-035 on the outbound transfer of systemic domestic capital. Systemic domestic capital includes critical
EO-SEC-17-036 infrastructure investments, advanced technology intellectual property, and high-volume digital liquidity.
EO-SEC-17-037 
EO-SEC-17-038 ### Section 34.2: Sender-Constrained Hardware-Attested Identity Links
EO-SEC-17-039 All high-value outbound capital transfers and cross-border digital asset transactions originating from
EO-SEC-17-040 United States financial institutions must utilize Sender-Constrained Hardware-Attested Identity Links (SCHAIL).
EO-SEC-17-041 1. **Hardware Attestation Requirements**: Transactions must be signed within a cryptographically secure,
EO-SEC-17-042    hardware-isolated environment, such as a Hardware Security Module (HSM), Trusted Platform Module (TPM),
EO-SEC-17-043    or processor-based Secure Enclaves (e.g., Intel SGX, AMD SEV, or Apple Secure Enclave).
EO-SEC-17-044 2. **Sender-Constrained Binding**: The cryptographic signature must bind the transaction payload directly
EO-SEC-17-045    to a verified, hardware-attested identity link, ensuring that the sender's identity cannot be spoofed,
EO-SEC-17-046    intercepted, or altered in transit by malicious actors or foreign adversaries.
EO-SEC-17-047 3. **Zero-Knowledge Proof Integration**: To preserve legitimate privacy rights while ensuring compliance,
EO-SEC-17-048    institutions may utilize zero-knowledge proofs (ZKPs) to verify hardware attestation and identity
EO-SEC-17-049    compliance without disclosing sensitive proprietary transaction details to unauthorized third parties.
EO-SEC-17-050 
EO-SEC-17-051 ### Section 34.3: Enforcement and Compliance Framework
EO-SEC-17-052 The Department of the Treasury, in coordination with the Department of Homeland Security and the Federal
EO-SEC-17-053 Reserve Board, shall oversee the implementation of the SCHAIL framework.
EO-SEC-17-054 1. Financial institutions failing to implement SCHAIL-compliant transaction verification within 180 days
EO-SEC-17-055    of this Order's issuance shall be subject to administrative penalties and restrictions on outbound transfers.
EO-SEC-17-056 2. The SEC's role in capital preservation is strictly limited to monitoring public disclosures of
EO-SEC-17-057    registered corporations, with no authority to regulate the underlying cryptographic hardware standards
EO-SEC-17-058    or the decentralized networks over which these transactions are executed.
EO-SEC-17-059 
EO-SEC-17-060 ## PART 33 & 34 UNIFICATION CLAUSE
EO-SEC-17-061 
EO-SEC-17-062 ### Section 35.1: Synthesis of Jurisdictional Limits and Capital Security
EO-SEC-17-063 The administrative firewall established in Part 33 and the capital preservation mandate in Part 34
EO-SEC-17-064 constitute a unified regulatory framework. By restricting the SEC's jurisdictional encroachment, this Order
EO-SEC-17-065 fosters a stable, predictable domestic environment for technological innovation. Simultaneously, the
EO-SEC-17-066 implementation of SCHAIL ensures that this open innovation environment does not compromise national security
EO-SEC-17-067 or facilitate illicit capital flight. The balance of clear agency boundaries and robust, hardware-level
EO-SEC-17-068 security protocols guarantees both economic freedom and national financial integrity.
