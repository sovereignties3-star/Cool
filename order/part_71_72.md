EO-SEC-36-001 # EXECUTIVE ORDER: INTEGRATED NATIONAL VOTER TRUST AND REGISTRY INTEGRITY INITIATIVE
EO-SEC-36-002 ## PART 71: ZERO-KNOWLEDGE BALLOT PROTECTION ACT AND PART 72: AUTOMATED REGISTRY COMPLIANCE SWEEP
EO-SEC-36-003 
EO-SEC-36-004 By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and the laws of the United States of America,
EO-SEC-36-005 including the Help America Vote Act (HAVA) and the National Voter Registration Act (NVRA), and in order to
EO-SEC-36-006 secure the cryptographic integrity of the democratic process while ensuring the absolute accuracy, privacy,
EO-SEC-36-007 and resilience of voter registration databases, it is hereby ordered as follows:
EO-SEC-36-008 
EO-SEC-36-009 ### SUBPART A - THE ZERO-KNOWLEDGE BALLOT PROTECTION ACT
EO-SEC-36-010 
EO-SEC-36-011 **Section 71.1. Establishment of the Cryptographic Ballot Verification Standard (CBVS).**
EO-SEC-36-012 (a) To protect the constitutional right to a secret ballot while providing verifiable proof of election
EO-SEC-36-013 integrity, the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), in coordination with the Cybersecurity
EO-SEC-36-014 and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA), shall establish the Cryptographic Ballot Verification Standard (CBVS).
EO-SEC-36-015 (b) The CBVS shall mandate the integration of Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKPs)—specifically non-interactive
EO-SEC-36-016 zero-knowledge arguments of knowledge (such as zk-SNARKs or zk-STARKs)—into all federally funded voting systems.
EO-SEC-36-017 (c) These cryptographic protocols must allow voters to verify that their individual ballots were cast-as-intended,
EO-SEC-36-018 recorded-as-cast, and counted-as-recorded, without revealing the voter's identity or specific selections to any
EO-SEC-36-019 third party, including election officials, system administrators, or external observers.
EO-SEC-36-020 
EO-SEC-36-021 **Section 71.2. Private Ballot Compilation and Decentralized Auditing.**
EO-SEC-36-022 (a) All jurisdictions receiving federal election assistance grants shall transition to private ballot compilation
EO-SEC-36-023 systems. These systems must compile aggregated election tallies mathematically using homomorphic encryption
EO-SEC-36-024 and zero-knowledge proofs, ensuring that the decryption of the final tally does not expose individual ballot data.
EO-SEC-36-025 (b) The Department of Homeland Security (DHS) shall establish the Federal Cryptographic Election Audit Board (FCEAB).
EO-SEC-36-026 The FCEAB will be tasked with providing open-source, mathematically verifiable auditing tools to state and local
EO-SEC-36-027 election authorities. These tools will allow independent public verification of the mathematical correctness of
EO-SEC-36-028 the election outcome without compromising voter anonymity.
EO-SEC-36-029 
EO-SEC-36-030 **Section 71.3. Implementation Timeline and Research Mandates.**
EO-SEC-36-031 (a) Within 180 days of this Order, the Director of NIST shall publish the preliminary technical specifications
EO-SEC-36-032 for zero-knowledge ballot compilation, including recommended elliptic curves and proof generation parameters.
EO-SEC-36-033 (b) The National Science Foundation (NSF) shall establish a dedicated research grant program to fund academic
EO-SEC-36-034 and private sector breakthroughs in low-latency, hardware-accelerated zero-knowledge proof generation for
EO-SEC-36-035 high-throughput municipal voting environments.

EO-SEC-36-037 ### SUBPART B - THE AUTOMATED REGISTRY COMPLIANCE SWEEP
EO-SEC-36-038 
EO-SEC-36-039 **Section 72.1. Automated Voter Database Maintenance and Standards.**
EO-SEC-36-040 (a) To ensure that voter registration rolls are accurate, secure, and consistently updated, there is established
EO-SEC-36-041 the Automated Registry Compliance Sweep (ARCS) protocol.
EO-SEC-36-042 (b) The Federal Election Commission (FEC), in consultation with the Social Security Administration (SSA), the
EO-SEC-36-043 Department of State, and state-level Departments of Motor Vehicles (DMVs), shall develop a secure, real-time
EO-SEC-36-044 data-sharing API. This API will facilitate the automated cross-referencing of voter registration databases
EO-SEC-36-045 to identify deceased voters, individuals who have permanently relocated out of the voting jurisdiction, and
EO-SEC-36-046 duplicate registrations across state lines.
EO-SEC-36-047 
EO-SEC-36-048 **Section 72.2. Safeguards Against Erroneous Voter Purging.**
EO-SEC-36-049 (a) To prevent the disenfranchisement of eligible citizens, any automated registry sweep must incorporate
EO-SEC-36-050 strict algorithmic safeguards. No voter shall be removed from a registration list based solely on a single
EO-SEC-36-051 automated database match.
EO-SEC-36-052 (b) The ARCS protocol requires a multi-factor verification system. A match must be confirmed across at least
EO-SEC-36-053 three independent, authoritative data sources (e.g., USPS National Change of Address database, SSA Death Master File,
EO-SEC-36-054 and state vital statistics registries) before any administrative action is initiated.
EO-SEC-36-055 (c) In the event of a confirmed match indicating potential ineligibility, the state election authority must
EO-SEC-36-056 issue a written notice via certified mail and electronic communication to the affected voter. The voter shall
EO-SEC-36-057 be granted a mandatory 90-day cure period to verify their registration status before any removal is finalized.
EO-SEC-36-058 (d) Any automated system used for registry sweeps must undergo independent bias and error-rate audits by CISA
EO-SEC-36-059 every 12 months. If an automated system exhibits a false-positive rate exceeding 0.01%, its authorization
EO-SEC-36-060 to perform automated sweeps shall be suspended immediately until corrective measures are certified.
EO-SEC-36-061 
EO-SEC-36-062 **Section 72.3. Integration of Cryptographic Verification and Registry Compliance.**
EO-SEC-36-063 (a) To bridge the Zero-Knowledge Ballot Protection Act and the Automated Registry Compliance Sweep, state
EO-SEC-36-064 election systems shall utilize zero-knowledge set membership proofs (such as Merkle Patricia Trees or
EO-SEC-36-065 cryptographic accumulators) to verify that a voter is on the active, compliant registry without exposing
EO-SEC-36-066 the voter's personally identifiable information (PII) during the check-in or ballot-casting process.
EO-SEC-36-067 (b) This integration ensures that while the registry is kept clean and compliant via automated sweeps, the
EO-SEC-36-068 act of voting remains completely private, secure, and mathematically insulated from administrative errors.
EO-SEC-36-069 
EO-SEC-36-070 **Section 72.4. Enforcement and Reporting.**
EO-SEC-36-071 (a) The Department of Justice (DOJ) Civil Rights Division shall monitor compliance with the safeguards
EO-SEC-36-072 established under this Part to ensure no systematic disenfranchisement occurs.
EO-SEC-36-073 (b) Each state participating in federal election funding shall submit an annual report to the Congress
EO-SEC-36-074 detailing the number of records updated, the number of notices sent, the number of registrations cured,
EO-SEC-36-075 and the cryptographic audit logs verifying the mathematical integrity of their voting systems.
