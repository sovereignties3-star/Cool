EO-SEC-23-001 | # EXECUTIVE ORDER: NATIONAL STRATEGY FOR ECONOMIC AND DIGITAL RESILIENCE
EO-SEC-23-002 | 
EO-SEC-23-003 | ## PART 45: FINANCIAL INNOVATION SAFE HARBOR EXTENSION (FISHE)
EO-SEC-23-004 | 
EO-SEC-23-005 | ### Section 45.1. Findings and Purpose
EO-SEC-23-006 | The Federal Government recognizes that financial technology (FinTech) and decentralized ledger systems are critical drivers of modern economic growth.
EO-SEC-23-007 | To foster sustainable innovation, it is necessary to provide stable, predictable regulatory environments for entities with proven compliance records.
EO-SEC-23-008 | Over-regulation of compliant actors stifles technological advancement and drives capital to offshore jurisdictions, undermining national security.
EO-SEC-23-009 | Therefore, this Section establishes an extended regulatory safe harbor for financial innovators demonstrating an exemplary "zero-fraud track record."
EO-SEC-23-010 | 
EO-SEC-23-011 | ### Section 45.2. Definitions
EO-SEC-23-012 | For the purposes of this Part, the following definitions shall apply:
EO-SEC-23-013 | (a) "Financial Innovator" means any individual, corporation, or decentralized autonomous organization engaged in developing novel financial products.
EO-SEC-23-014 | (b) "Zero-Fraud Track Record" means a continuous period of twenty-four (24) months with zero substantiated consumer fraud complaints, zero regulatory sanctions,
EO-SEC-23-015 | and zero material misstatements in public disclosures, as verified by the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) and the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC).
EO-SEC-23-016 | (c) "Safe Harbor Extension" means a legally binding deferral of non-essential compliance audits and a shield against retroactive civil enforcement actions.
EO-SEC-23-017 | 
EO-SEC-23-018 | ### Section 45.3. Safe Harbor Eligibility and Application Process
EO-SEC-23-019 | Any Financial Innovator may petition the newly established Interagency Financial Innovation Council (IFIC) for Safe Harbor status.
EO-SEC-23-020 | The petition must include audited transaction ledgers, independent third-party security audits, and a certified compliance history.
EO-SEC-23-021 | Upon receipt of the petition, the IFIC shall conduct a comprehensive review of the applicant's operational history within forty-five (45) business days.
EO-SEC-23-022 | If the applicant meets the criteria for a Zero-Fraud Track Record, the IFIC shall issue a Certificate of Safe Harbor Extension, valid for three (3) fiscal years.
EO-SEC-23-023 | 
EO-SEC-23-024 | ### Section 45.4. Scope of Safe Harbor Protections
EO-SEC-23-025 | Entities holding a valid Certificate of Safe Harbor Extension shall be entitled to the following statutory protections:
EO-SEC-23-026 | (a) Immunity from civil enforcement actions by federal regulatory agencies regarding novel asset classifications, provided the entity acts in good faith.
EO-SEC-23-027 | (b) Exemption from redundant state-level money transmitter licensing requirements, preempted under federal commerce clause authority.
EO-SEC-23-028 | (c) Access to a dedicated regulatory sandbox liaison to facilitate real-time compliance adjustments without penalization or public disclosure of minor infractions.
EO-SEC-23-029 | 
EO-SEC-23-030 | ### Section 45.5. Revocation and Penalties
EO-SEC-23-031 | The Safe Harbor Extension shall be immediately revoked if the holder is found to have committed intentional fraud, market manipulation, or systemic negligence.
EO-SEC-23-032 | Upon revocation, the entity shall be subject to standard regulatory oversight and may face expedited civil penalties, including permanent debarment.
EO-SEC-23-033 | 
EO-SEC-23-034 | ## PART 46: STATUTORY ANTI-DEPLATFORMING SHIELD (SADS)
EO-SEC-23-035 | 
EO-SEC-23-036 | ### Section 46.1. Findings and Purpose
EO-SEC-23-037 | Professional livelihoods are increasingly dependent on access to digital infrastructure, communication networks, and corporate hosting platforms.
EO-SEC-23-038 | Arbitrary, politically motivated, or algorithmically driven deplatforming of professionals undermines economic stability and individual liberty.
EO-SEC-23-039 | The Federal Government has a compelling interest in protecting the digital identities and professional profiles of its citizens from unilateral corporate censorship.
EO-SEC-23-040 | This Section establishes robust legal protections to shield professional profiles on corporate platforms from arbitrary termination or restriction.
EO-SEC-23-041 | 
EO-SEC-23-042 | ### Section 46.2. Definitions
EO-SEC-23-043 | For the purposes of this Part, the following definitions shall apply:
EO-SEC-23-044 | (a) "Corporate Platform" means any digital service provider, social network, code repository, or cloud host with more than ten million (10,000,000) active users.
EO-SEC-23-045 | (b) "Professional Profile" means a digital account primarily used for career networking, software development, business operations, or professional communication.
EO-SEC-23-046 | (c) "Deplatforming" means the permanent suspension, shadow-banning, or severe restriction of a Professional Profile without clear, documented cause.
EO-SEC-23-047 | 
EO-SEC-23-048 | ### Section 46.3. Mandatory Due Process Requirements
EO-SEC-23-049 | No Corporate Platform shall deplatform a Professional Profile without adhering to the following mandatory due process standards:
EO-SEC-23-050 | (a) **Written Notice**: The platform must provide the user with a detailed, written explanation of the specific Terms of Service (ToS) violated, including evidence.
EO-SEC-23-051 | (b) **Pre-Suspension Grace Period**: Except in cases of imminent physical harm or verified criminal activity, platforms must provide a fourteen (14) day grace period.
EO-SEC-23-052 | During this grace period, the user shall have the opportunity to appeal the decision, download their professional data, and correct any alleged violations.
EO-SEC-23-053 | (c) **Independent Appeal Mechanism**: Platforms must establish an independent, human-reviewed appeal process that resolves disputes within seven (7) business days.
EO-SEC-23-054 | 
EO-SEC-23-055 | ### Section 46.4. Professional Profile Protection Standards
EO-SEC-23-056 | Corporate Platforms are strictly prohibited from restricting access to Professional Profiles based on:
EO-SEC-23-057 | (a) Political affiliation, ideological viewpoints, or lawful off-platform speech and activities.
EO-SEC-23-058 | (b) Participation in legally protected whistleblowing activities or public interest disclosures.
EO-SEC-23-059 | (c) Algorithmic flags that have not been verified by a qualified human moderator within forty-eight (48) hours of the initial flag.
EO-SEC-23-060 | 
EO-SEC-23-061 | ### Section 46.5. Civil Remedies and Enforcement
EO-SEC-23-062 | Any professional whose profile is unlawfully restricted or terminated in violation of this Part may bring a civil action in federal district court.
EO-SEC-23-063 | Remedies shall include:
EO-SEC-23-064 | (a) Statutory damages of up to ten thousand dollars ($10,000) per day for each day the Professional Profile remains unlawfully restricted.
EO-SEC-23-065 | (b) Injunctive relief requiring the immediate reinstatement of the profile and restoration of all associated data and network connections.
EO-SEC-23-066 | (c) Reasonable attorney's fees and litigation costs incurred by the prevailing plaintiff.
EO-SEC-23-067 | The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) shall maintain a public registry of complaints and issue annual compliance ratings for all covered Corporate Platforms.
EO-SEC-23-068 | 
EO-SEC-23-069 | ## PART 45 & 46: UNIFIED IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS
EO-SEC-23-070 | 
EO-SEC-23-071 | ### Section 47.1. Interagency Coordination and Data Sharing
EO-SEC-23-072 | To ensure the seamless integration of the Financial Innovation Safe Harbor and the Statutory Anti-Deplatforming Shield, the IFIC and the FTC shall establish
EO-SEC-23-073 | a secure, real-time data-sharing protocol. This protocol will monitor corporate compliance and track the intersection of financial and digital exclusion.
EO-SEC-23-074 | 
EO-SEC-23-075 | ### Section 47.2. Severability
EO-SEC-23-076 | If any provision of this Order, or the application of such provision to any person or circumstance, is held to be invalid, the remainder of this Order
EO-SEC-23-077 | and the application of its provisions to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
