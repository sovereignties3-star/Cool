EO-SEC-04-001 # EXECUTIVE ORDER: INTEGRATED FINANCIAL SYSTEM INTEGRITY AND SECURITY
EO-SEC-04-002 ## PART VII & VIII: ANTI-ARBITRAGE LOCKS AND ASYMMETRIC CRYPTOGRAPHIC HANDSHAKES
EO-SEC-04-003 
EO-SEC-04-004 ### SECTION 1: PREAMBLE AND STATUTORY AUTHORITY
EO-SEC-04-005 By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and the laws of the United States of America,
EO-SEC-04-006 including the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (50 U.S.C. 1701 et seq.), the National Emergencies Act
EO-SEC-04-007 (50 U.S.C. 1601 et seq.), and section 301 of title 3, United States Code, it is hereby ordered as follows:
EO-SEC-04-008 This order establishes critical protocols to secure the national financial infrastructure against systemic
EO-SEC-04-009 vulnerabilities arising from high-frequency arbitrage, fractional secondary ledger creation, and latency
EO-SEC-04-010 differentials between legacy banking networks and modern distributed ledger technologies.
EO-SEC-04-011 
EO-SEC-04-012 ### SECTION 2: PART VII - THE ANTI-ARBITRAGE LOCK (AAL) PROTOCOL
EO-SEC-04-013 (a) **Prevention of Fractional Secondary Ledgers.** Financial institutions operating within the jurisdiction
EO-SEC-04-014 of the United States are strictly prohibited from establishing, maintaining, or clearing transactions through
EO-SEC-04-015 unbacked, fractional secondary ledgers. Any secondary ledger or off-chain scaling solution must maintain
EO-SEC-04-016 a strict 1:1 fully-reserved cryptographic peg to the primary underlying asset at all times.
EO-SEC-04-017 (b) **Implementation of the Anti-Arbitrage Lock (AAL).** To enforce this prohibition, the Department of the
EO-SEC-04-018 Treasury, in coordination with the Federal Reserve System, shall mandate the integration of an automated
EO-SEC-04-019 Anti-Arbitrage Lock (AAL) mechanism into all licensed digital asset custody and settlement platforms.
EO-SEC-04-020 (i) The AAL shall programmatically freeze assets on the primary ledger immediately upon the initiation of
EO-SEC-04-021 any secondary ledger transaction, preventing double-spend, rehypothecation, or fractional lending.
EO-SEC-04-022 (ii) The lock must remain active until cryptographic proof of final settlement is broadcast and verified
EO-SEC-04-023 on the primary ledger, eliminating the settlement window traditionally exploited for arbitrage.
EO-SEC-04-024 (c) **Auditability and Real-Time Reporting.** All institutions utilizing secondary ledgers must expose
EO-SEC-04-025 read-only cryptographic APIs to the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) and the Securities and
EO-SEC-04-026 Exchange Commission (SEC) to allow continuous, real-time verification of 1:1 asset backing.
EO-SEC-04-027 
EO-SEC-04-028 ### SECTION 3: PART VIII - ASYMMETRIC CRYPTOGRAPHIC HANDSHAKES (ACH)
EO-SEC-04-029 (a) **Mitigation of Legacy Banking Network Latency.** Legacy payment systems, including the Automated Clearing
EO-SEC-04-030 House (ACH) and the Society for Worldwide Interbank Financial Telecommunication (SWIFT), exhibit inherent
EO-SEC-04-031 latency delays ranging from several hours to multiple business days. This latency introduces significant
EO-SEC-04-032 counterparty risk and systemic vulnerability when interfacing with real-time cryptographic ledgers.
EO-SEC-04-033 (b) **Establishment of Asymmetric Cryptographic Handshakes.** To bridge this latency gap securely, all clearing
EO-SEC-04-034 banks operating legacy-to-cryptographic gateways shall implement Asymmetric Cryptographic Handshakes (ACH).
EO-SEC-04-035 (i) The ACH protocol utilizes asymmetric key pairs to generate time-locked, conditional transaction commitments.
EO-SEC-04-036 (ii) Upon initiation of a legacy transfer, the gateway bank shall sign a cryptographic commitment using its
EO-SEC-04-037 private key, locking the equivalent value in a secure, smart-contract-controlled escrow on the destination ledger.
EO-SEC-04-038 (iii) This commitment acts as a legally binding, cryptographically verifiable guarantee of funds, allowing the
EO-SEC-04-039 receiving party to safely interact with the transaction state prior to final legacy settlement.
EO-SEC-04-040 (c) **Latency-Arbitrage Protection.** The ACH protocol must incorporate a dynamic fee-adjustment and slashing
EO-SEC-04-041 mechanism. If a legacy transaction is cancelled, reversed, or fails to settle within the designated time-lock
EO-SEC-04-042 window, the initiating institution's escrowed collateral shall be partially slashed to compensate the counterparty
EO-SEC-04-043 for liquidity lockup and potential market movement during the latency period.
EO-SEC-04-044 
EO-SEC-04-045 ### SECTION 4: UNIFIED SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE AND INTEROPERABILITY
EO-SEC-04-046 (a) **The Unified Settlement Interface.** The AAL and ACH protocols shall be unified into a single, cohesive
EO-SEC-04-047 technical standard known as the Unified Settlement Interface (USI). The USI will serve as the national
EO-SEC-04-048 standard for secure cross-network financial transactions.
EO-SEC-04-049 (b) **Technical Specifications.** The USI must adhere to the following technical requirements:
EO-SEC-04-050 (i) Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKPs) must be utilized to verify the solvency of secondary ledgers without
EO-SEC-04-051 exposing proprietary transaction data, customer identities, or trading strategies.
EO-SEC-04-052 (ii) Multi-Party Computation (MPC) must be employed for the management of the asymmetric key pairs used
EO-SEC-04-053 in the ACH protocol, ensuring no single point of failure exists within the gateway architecture.
EO-SEC-04-054 (iii) Latency-monitoring nodes must be distributed geographically to dynamically calculate optimal time-lock
EO-SEC-04-055 durations based on real-time network congestion and legacy clearing house performance metrics.
EO-SEC-04-056 
EO-SEC-04-057 ### SECTION 5: RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT DIRECTIVES
EO-SEC-04-058 (a) **Joint Task Force.** The Director of the Office of Science and Technology Policy (OSTP), in collaboration
EO-SEC-04-059 with the Chairman of the Federal Reserve and the Secretary of the Treasury, shall establish a Joint Task Force
EO-SEC-04-060 on Cryptographic Financial Infrastructure.
EO-SEC-04-061 (b) **Mandated Research Areas.** The Task Force shall conduct research and publish standards on:
EO-SEC-04-062 (i) The mathematical prevention of fractional reserve practices in off-chain state channels and rollups.
EO-SEC-04-063 (ii) The optimization of cryptographic handshake verification times on legacy hardware architectures.
EO-SEC-04-064 (iii) The resilience of the USI against quantum computing threats, including the transition to post-quantum
EO-SEC-04-065 cryptography (PQC) standards for all asymmetric handshakes.
EO-SEC-04-066 
EO-SEC-04-067 ### SECTION 6: COMPLIANCE, PENALTIES, AND ENFORCEMENT
EO-SEC-04-068 (a) **Compliance Timeline.** All covered financial institutions must achieve full compliance with the AAL
EO-SEC-04-069 and ACH protocols within 180 days of the issuance of this order.
EO-SEC-04-070 (b) **Civil and Criminal Penalties.** Failure to comply with the provisions of this order, or the deliberate
EO-SEC-04-071 evasion of the AAL through the creation of unauthorized fractional secondary ledgers, shall be subject to
EO-SEC-04-072 civil penalties of up to $1,000,000 per violation per day, as well as criminal prosecution under applicable
EO-SEC-04-073 federal statutes for officers and directors who knowingly authorize non-compliant transactions.
EO-SEC-04-074 (c) **Enforcement Agency.** The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) and the Consumer Financial
EO-SEC-04-075 Protection Bureau (CFPB) shall have joint enforcement authority over retail-facing institutions, while the
EO-SEC-04-076 Federal Reserve shall oversee wholesale and systemic clearing institutions.
EO-SEC-04-077 
EO-SEC-04-078 ### SECTION 7: DEFINITIONS
EO-SEC-04-079 For the purposes of this order:
EO-SEC-04-080 (a) "Anti-Arbitrage Lock" means a cryptographic mechanism that prevents the transfer or utilization of an asset
EO-SEC-04-081 on a primary ledger while a corresponding transaction is pending or active on a secondary ledger.
EO-SEC-04-082 (b) "Asymmetric Cryptographic Handshake" means a protocol utilizing public-key cryptography to establish secure,
EO-SEC-04-083 verifiable, and time-locked commitments between high-speed ledgers and high-latency legacy networks.
EO-SEC-04-084 (c) "Fractional Secondary Ledger" means any ledger, database, or record-keeping system that represents claims
EO-SEC-04-085 on an underlying asset without maintaining a 1:1, fully-reserved, cryptographically locked balance of that asset.
EO-SEC-04-086 (d) "Legacy Banking Network" means traditional financial clearing systems including, but not limited to, Fedwire,
EO-SEC-04-087 ACH, SWIFT, and corresponding interbank clearing houses.
