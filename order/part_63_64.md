EO-SEC-32-001 # EXECUTIVE ORDER: INTEGRATION OF PERSONAL SOVEREIGNTY AND BORDER VALIDATION
EO-SEC-32-002 ## SUBCHAPTER XXXII - SECURE TRANSIT AND DECENTRALIZED IDENTITY
EO-SEC-32-003 
EO-SEC-32-004 This document establishes the unified operational and technical standards for the integration of the Personal Sovereignty Access Portal (PSAP) and the Automated Border Validation Interface (ABVI).
EO-SEC-32-005 
EO-SEC-32-006 ### PART 63: PERSONAL SOVEREIGNTY ACCESS PORTAL (PSAP)
EO-SEC-32-007 
EO-SEC-32-008 #### Section 63.1: Purpose and Establishment
EO-SEC-32-009 1. The Personal Sovereignty Access Portal (PSAP) is hereby established as the official, citizen-centric decentralized identity gateway.
EO-SEC-32-010 2. The PSAP shall empower individuals with absolute ownership over their digital identity, credentials, and personal data attributes.
EO-SEC-32-011 3. All federal agencies shall interface with the PSAP as the primary mechanism for verifying individual identity claims.
EO-SEC-32-012 
EO-SEC-32-013 #### Section 63.2: Personal Key Pairings and Cryptographic Architecture
EO-SEC-32-014 1. **Cryptographic Foundation**: The PSAP shall utilize asymmetric cryptography to generate unique, user-controlled personal key pairings.
EO-SEC-32-015    - **Algorithm Standards**: Key pairings must conform to FIPS 186-5 standards, utilizing Ed25519 (Edwards-curve Digital Signature Algorithm) or ECDSA over secp256r1/secp256k1 curves.
EO-SEC-32-016    - **Hardware Isolation**: Private keys must be generated and stored within a hardware-secured environment, such as a Trusted Platform Module (TPM) or a mobile device's Secure Enclave.
EO-SEC-32-017    - **Zero-Knowledge Custody**: Under no circumstances shall any federal agency, contractor, or third party have access to, custody of, or visibility into an individual's private key.
EO-SEC-32-018 2. **Decentralized Identifiers (DIDs)**:
EO-SEC-32-019    - Each personal key pairing shall resolve to a W3C-compliant Decentralized Identifier (DID).
EO-SEC-32-020    - The DID document shall contain only the public key material and cryptographic verification relationships, completely devoid of personally identifiable information (PII).
EO-SEC-32-021 3. **Verifiable Credentials (VCs)**:
EO-SEC-32-022    - Government-issued attributes (e.g., citizenship, date of birth, security clearances) shall be issued as signed Verifiable Credentials.
EO-SEC-32-023    - VCs must support selective disclosure mechanisms, specifically utilizing BBS+ Signatures to allow individuals to prove specific attributes (e.g., "Age > 21") without revealing their exact birthdate.
EO-SEC-32-024 
EO-SEC-32-025 ### PART 64: AUTOMATED BORDER VALIDATION INTERFACE (ABVI)
EO-SEC-32-026 
EO-SEC-32-027 #### Section 64.1: Purpose and Deployment
EO-SEC-32-028 1. The Automated Border Validation Interface (ABVI) is established to modernize, secure, and accelerate international and domestic transit checkpoints.
EO-SEC-32-029 2. The ABVI shall replace legacy physical document checks with automated, cryptographically secure validation gates.
EO-SEC-32-030 3. The ABVI must operate in direct, real-time synchronization with the PSAP framework to verify traveler credentials.
EO-SEC-32-031 
EO-SEC-32-032 #### Section 64.2: Cryptographic Border Attestation Protocols
EO-SEC-32-033 1. **Attestation Generation**:
EO-SEC-32-034    - Prior to arrival at a transit checkpoint, the traveler's PSAP client shall generate a localized, ephemeral Cryptographic Border Attestation (CBA).
EO-SEC-32-035    - The CBA shall consist of a time-bound, single-use cryptographic proof signed by the traveler's private key.
EO-SEC-32-036    - The CBA must incorporate a challenge nonce issued by the ABVI terminal to prevent replay attacks.
EO-SEC-32-037 2. **Zero-Knowledge Transit Verification**:
EO-SEC-32-038    - The ABVI terminal shall verify the CBA using Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKPs), specifically zk-SNARKs (Zero-Knowledge Succinct Non-Interactive Arguments of Knowledge).
EO-SEC-32-039    - This verification must confirm:
EO-SEC-32-040      - The traveler possesses a valid, unrevoked passport credential issued by an authorized authority.
EO-SEC-32-041      - The traveler is not on any active restricted transit registries.
EO-SEC-32-042      - The traveler's identity matches the biometric hash bound to the credential (verified locally on-device via secure biometric match).
EO-SEC-32-043    - No raw biometric data, names, or passport numbers shall be transmitted to or stored by the ABVI terminal during this process.
EO-SEC-32-044 3. **Decentralized Public Key Infrastructure (DPKI)**:
EO-SEC-32-045    - The ABVI shall query a globally distributed, federated ledger of public keys belonging to authorized issuing authorities (e.g., Department of State, international partners).
EO-SEC-32-046    - This DPKI registry shall be updated in real-time to ensure immediate revocation of compromised issuing keys.
EO-SEC-32-047 
EO-SEC-32-048 ### PART 63 & 64 UNIFIED INTEGRATION SPECIFICATION
EO-SEC-32-049 
EO-SEC-32-050 #### Section 65.1: End-to-End Transit Workflow
EO-SEC-32-051 1. **Step 1: Pre-Clearance Request**
EO-SEC-32-052    - The traveler initiates a transit request via their PSAP application.
EO-SEC-32-053    - The PSAP app requests a cryptographic challenge from the ABVI network.
EO-SEC-32-054 2. **Step 2: Challenge and Proof Generation**
EO-SEC-32-055    - The ABVI network returns a cryptographically secure random nonce.
EO-SEC-32-056    - The traveler's device signs the nonce along with the required Verifiable Credentials using the hardware-bound private key.
EO-SEC-32-057 3. **Step 3: Gate Presentation**
EO-SEC-32-058    - The traveler presents the generated QR code or transmits the payload via Near Field Communication (NFC) to the ABVI physical gate.
EO-SEC-32-059 4. **Step 4: Cryptographic Attestation Validation**
EO-SEC-32-060    - The ABVI gate validates the signature against the public key registered in the DPKI.
EO-SEC-32-061    - The gate executes the ZKP verification protocol to confirm eligibility.
EO-SEC-32-062 5. **Step 5: Access Grant and Ephemeral Logging**
EO-SEC-32-063    - Upon successful validation, the gate opens automatically.
EO-SEC-32-064    - An ephemeral, non-associative log of the transaction (containing only timestamp, gate ID, and cryptographic success proof) is recorded.
EO-SEC-32-065    - All session data on the ABVI terminal is immediately purged from volatile memory.
EO-SEC-32-066 
EO-SEC-32-067 #### Section 65.2: Security and Compliance Mandates
EO-SEC-32-068 1. **Auditability**: All ABVI terminals must undergo continuous, automated cryptographic audits to ensure no leakage of private keys or PII occurs.
EO-SEC-32-069 2. **Fail-Safe Protocols**: In the event of a network partition or DPKI outage, ABVI terminals must fall back to a localized, offline verification mode using cached public keys, ensuring zero disruption to critical transit infrastructure.
EO-SEC-32-070 3. **Interoperability**: The PSAP and ABVI protocols must remain fully compliant with international standards, including ISO/IEC 18013-5 (Mobile Driving License) and ICAO Doc 9303 (Machine Readable Travel Documents).
