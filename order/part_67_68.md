EO-SEC-34-001 # EXECUTIVE ORDER: INTEGRATED NATIONAL CYBER DEFENSE FRAMEWORK
EO-SEC-34-002 ## PART 67 & PART 68: UNIFIED IDENTITY PROTECTION AND SYNTHETIC PRESENCE MITIGATION
EO-SEC-34-003 
EO-SEC-34-004 ### SECTION 1: PURPOSE AND AUTHORITY
EO-SEC-34-005 This joint directive establishes a unified cryptographic and hardware-attested defense perimeter,
EO-SEC-34-006 merging the Post-Quantum Identity Encryption Wall (Part 67) and the Anti-Synthetic Presence Protocol (Part 68).
EO-SEC-34-007 Under the authority vested in the President by the Constitution and the laws of the United States,
EO-SEC-34-008 including the Cybersecurity Enhancement Act of 2014 and the Federal Information Security Modernization Act (FISMA),
EO-SEC-34-009 it is hereby ordered that federal information systems transition to quantum-resistant identity frameworks
EO-SEC-34-010 and implement mandatory hardware-attested live presence validation to counter synthetic intelligence threats.
EO-SEC-34-011 
EO-SEC-34-012 ### SECTION 2: PART 67 - POST-QUANTUM IDENTITY ENCRYPTION WALL (PQ-IEW)
EO-SEC-34-013 (a) **Mandate for Quantum-Resistant Cryptography**: All executive departments and agencies shall immediately
EO-SEC-34-014 initiate the deprecation of public-key cryptography standards vulnerable to quantum computing architectures,
EO-SEC-34-015 specifically targeting RSA, ECDSA, and Diffie-Hellman algorithms currently utilized for identity verification,
EO-SEC-34-016 credentialing, and access control.
EO-SEC-34-017 
EO-SEC-34-018 (b) **Approved Post-Quantum Algorithms**: Agencies shall transition all identity infrastructure to the
EO-SEC-34-019 National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) approved post-quantum cryptographic standards:
EO-SEC-34-020 (1) **ML-KEM (FIPS 203)**: For secure key encapsulation mechanisms during identity session establishment.
EO-SEC-34-021 (2) **ML-DSA (FIPS 204)**: For primary digital signatures and identity credential verification.
EO-SEC-34-022 (3) **SLH-DSA (FIPS 205)**: For stateless hash-based signatures as a secondary fallback mechanism.
EO-SEC-34-023 
EO-SEC-34-024 (c) **Zero-Knowledge Identity Verification**: To minimize the exposure of Personally Identifiable Information (PII),
EO-SEC-34-025 the Federal Chief Information Officer (CIO) shall deploy Zero-Knowledge Proof (ZKP) protocols.
EO-SEC-34-026 These protocols must allow citizens and federal employees to authenticate identity claims (e.g., age, clearance,
EO-SEC-34-027 citizenship) without transmitting the underlying plaintext attributes across network boundaries.
EO-SEC-34-028 
EO-SEC-34-029 (d) **Implementation Timeline**:
EO-SEC-34-030 (1) Within 180 days of this Order, agencies shall complete an inventory of all identity-related cryptographic assets.
EO-SEC-34-031 (2) Within 360 days, all external-facing federal identity portals must support hybrid classical/post-quantum
EO-SEC-34-032 cryptographic handshakes.
EO-SEC-34-033 (3) Within 720 days, all internal federal identity directories must operate exclusively on post-quantum algorithms.
EO-SEC-34-034 
EO-SEC-34-035 ### SECTION 3: PART 68 - ANTI-SYNTHETIC PRESENCE PROTOCOL (ASPP)
EO-SEC-34-036 (a) **Establishment of Live Presence Validation**: To combat the proliferation of generative artificial intelligence,
EO-SEC-34-037 deepfakes, and synthetic identity injection attacks, all remote identity verifications must utilize
EO-SEC-34-038 hardware-attested live presence validation.
EO-SEC-34-039 
EO-SEC-34-040 (b) **Hardware-Level Cryptographic Attestation**:
EO-SEC-34-041 (1) **Root of Trust**: Verification systems must interface directly with hardware-based Roots of Trust,
EO-SEC-34-042 including Trusted Platform Modules (TPM 2.0) and Secure Execution Environments (SEEs) embedded in user devices.
EO-SEC-34-043 (2) **Sensor-to-Enclave Binding**: Biometric capture devices (cameras, microphones) must cryptographically sign
EO-SEC-34-044 raw sensor data within a secure enclave before transmission. The private key used for signing must be
EO-SEC-34-045 hardware-bound and inaccessible to the host operating system or any user-space applications.
EO-SEC-34-046 (3) **Remote Attestation**: The verification server must validate the hardware signature and the integrity
EO-SEC-34-047 of the secure enclave's state using remote attestation protocols prior to processing any biometric data.
EO-SEC-34-048 
EO-SEC-34-049 (c) **Multi-Modal Liveness Detection**:
EO-SEC-34-050 (1) **Passive Liveness**: Systems must analyze high-resolution spatial-temporal features to detect synthetic
EO-SEC-34-051 texture patterns, inconsistent lighting, and unnatural physiological micro-movements.
EO-SEC-34-052 (2) **Active Liveness**: Systems must issue dynamic, unpredictable challenges (e.g., randomized focal shifts,
EO-SEC-34-053 specific phrase vocalization, or structured light projection) to verify real-time physical presence.
EO-SEC-34-054 
EO-SEC-34-055 ### SECTION 4: UNIFIED ARCHITECTURAL INTEGRATION
EO-SEC-34-056 (a) **The Quantum-Attested Identity Tunnel**: The PQ-IEW and ASPP shall be integrated into a single,
EO-SEC-34-057 end-to-end secure authentication pipeline.
EO-SEC-34-058 (1) The hardware-attested live presence metadata generated under Section 3 must be encapsulated within
EO-SEC-34-059 a post-quantum encrypted payload using ML-KEM.
EO-SEC-34-060 (2) The resulting payload must be digitally signed using ML-DSA by the device's secure enclave,
EO-SEC-34-061 establishing a mathematically unbreakable link between the physical user, the hardware device, and the
EO-SEC-34-062 post-quantum identity credential.
EO-SEC-34-063 
EO-SEC-34-064 (b) **Zero-Trust Policy Enforcement**:
EO-SEC-34-065 (1) No remote access to high-value federal assets (Impact Level 4 and above) shall be granted without
EO-SEC-34-066 a verified, hardware-attested live presence token.
EO-SEC-34-067 (2) Any detection of synthetic media injection, enclave compromise, or cryptographic degradation
EO-SEC-34-068 shall trigger an immediate, automated revocation of the session and initiate an incident response protocol.
EO-SEC-34-069 
EO-SEC-34-070 ### SECTION 5: COMPLIANCE, AUDITING, AND STANDARDS
EO-SEC-34-071 (a) **Continuous Monitoring**: The Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) shall establish
EO-SEC-34-072 a continuous monitoring framework to audit agency compliance with both PQ-IEW and ASPP standards.
EO-SEC-34-073 (b) **Interoperability Standards**: NIST shall publish unified technical specifications within 120 days
EO-SEC-34-074 detailing the exact API schemas, cryptographic formats, and hardware attestation protocols required
EO-SEC-34-075 to achieve cross-agency interoperability.
EO-SEC-34-076 (c) **Privacy Safeguards**: All implementations must adhere to strict privacy-by-design principles.
EO-SEC-34-077 Biometric templates and raw sensor data must never be stored centrally; they must be processed ephemerally
EO-SEC-34-078 within secure enclaves or discarded immediately following successful liveness and identity verification.
EO-SEC-34-079 
EO-SEC-34-080 ### SECTION 6: DEFINITIONS
EO-SEC-34-081 For the purposes of this Order:
EO-SEC-34-082 (a) "Post-Quantum Cryptography" means cryptographic algorithms that are analyzed to be secure against
EO-SEC-34-083 both quantum and classical computers.
EO-SEC-34-084 (b) "Hardware-Attested Live Presence" means a state of physical existence verified through cryptographic
EO-SEC-34-085 signatures generated by a secure hardware enclave linked directly to physical capture sensors.
EO-SEC-34-086 (c) "Synthetic Identity" means an identity created using fabricated or modified biometric and demographic data,
EO-SEC-34-087 often generated by artificial intelligence models.
EO-SEC-34-088 (d) "Secure Enclave" means an isolated, hardware-based security component that is independent of the main
EO-SEC-34-089 processor and operating system, designed to protect sensitive cryptographic keys and biometric data.
