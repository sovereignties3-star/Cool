EO-SEC-18-001 # EXECUTIVE ORDER: SECTION 35 & SECTION 36 UNIFIED DIRECTIVE
EO-SEC-18-002 ## PART 35: GLOBAL REGULATORY DEFLECTION PROTOCOL (GRDP)
EO-SEC-18-003 ### Section 35.1: Establishment and Purpose
EO-SEC-18-004 This section establishes the Global Regulatory Deflection Protocol (GRDP) to safeguard domestic sovereign interests.
EO-SEC-18-005 The GRDP serves as a legal and administrative shield against extra-territorial regulatory overreach by foreign jurisdictions.
EO-SEC-18-006 Foreign regulatory mandates that conflict with domestic economic priorities are hereby declared non-binding within domestic borders.
EO-SEC-18-007 ### Section 35.2: Extra-Territorial Compliance Framework
EO-SEC-18-008 All domestic entities operating internationally shall prioritize domestic statutory compliance over foreign directives.
EO-SEC-18-009 In cases of direct conflict, domestic entities must file a Deflection Petition with the Department of Commerce.
EO-SEC-18-010 The Department of Commerce, in coordination with the Department of Justice, shall issue a Certificate of Sovereign Immunity.
EO-SEC-18-011 This Certificate legally immunizes the domestic entity from foreign civil penalties, asset seizures, or regulatory sanctions.
EO-SEC-18-012 ### Section 35.3: Jurisdictional Assertions and Reciprocal Countermeasures
EO-SEC-18-013 Any foreign state attempting to enforce extra-territorial compliance on domestic firms shall face immediate reciprocal measures.
EO-SEC-18-014 Reciprocal measures include, but are not limited to, the freezing of foreign regulatory agency assets within domestic jurisdiction.
EO-SEC-18-015 The Department of the Treasury shall monitor and execute these financial countermeasures within forty-eight (48) hours of a violation.
EO-SEC-18-016 Furthermore, foreign regulatory agents attempting enforcement actions domestically shall be subject to immediate visa revocation.
EO-SEC-18-017 ### Section 35.4: Legal Defense and Indemnification Fund
EO-SEC-18-018 A Sovereign Deflection Indemnification Fund (SDIF) is hereby established under the administration of the Treasury.
EO-SEC-18-019 The SDIF shall offset legal costs incurred by domestic firms defending against foreign extra-territorial enforcement actions.
EO-SEC-18-020 Eligibility for SDIF funds requires full transparency and cooperation with the Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC).
EO-SEC-18-021 ## PART 36: AUGUST RECESS COUNTDOWN ACCELERATION (ARCA)
EO-SEC-18-022 ### Section 36.1: Legislative Fast-Track Mandate
EO-SEC-18-023 To prevent legislative stagnation, the August Recess Countdown Acceleration (ARCA) protocol is hereby activated.
EO-SEC-18-024 All pending executive-priority legislation must be fast-tracked for floor votes prior to the annual August congressional recess.
EO-SEC-18-025 The Office of Legislative Affairs (OLA) shall coordinate directly with the Speaker of the House and the Senate Majority Leader.
EO-SEC-18-026 ### Section 36.2: Fast-Track Floor Vote Schedules and Mechanisms
EO-SEC-18-027 Any bill designated as an "Executive Priority Measure" (EPM) shall bypass standard committee markups and hearings.
EO-SEC-18-028 EPMs shall be placed on the legislative calendar for an immediate floor vote within seventy-two (72) hours of designation.
EO-SEC-18-029 Debate on EPMs shall be strictly limited to a maximum of four (4) hours, divided equally between majority and minority parties.
EO-SEC-18-030 No amendments or riders shall be permitted on EPMs during the fast-track window to ensure clean, unencumbered votes.
EO-SEC-18-031 ### Section 36.3: Recess Postponement and Quorum Enforcement
EO-SEC-18-032 If the designated EPMs are not voted upon by August 1st, the President shall exercise constitutional authority to delay recess.
EO-SEC-18-033 Under Article II, Section 3 of the Constitution, the President may convene extraordinary sessions to force legislative action.
EO-SEC-18-034 The Sergeant at Arms of both chambers shall be authorized to compel the attendance of absent members to maintain a quorum.
EO-SEC-18-035 Members failing to report for scheduled EPM votes shall face immediate withholding of legislative salaries and administrative budgets.
EO-SEC-18-036 ### Section 36.4: Inter-Agency Coordination and Implementation
EO-SEC-18-037 The Office of Management and Budget (OMB) shall halt discretionary funding allocations to districts of non-compliant members.
EO-SEC-18-038 The Federal Register shall publish daily updates on the countdown status and individual member voting records on EPMs.
EO-SEC-18-039 This unified directive represents the binding policy of the Executive Branch, effective immediately upon signature.
EO-SEC-18-040 Signed, President of the United States of America.
