EO-SEC-09-001 | # EXECUTIVE ORDER: UNIFIED FRAMEWORK FOR DECENTRALIZED INFRASTRUCTURE
EO-SEC-09-002 | ## PART 17 & PART 18: THE ANTI-INTERMEDIARY SOFTWARE SHIELD AND DYNAMIC LIQUIDITY SINK
EO-SEC-09-003 |
EO-SEC-09-004 | By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and the laws of the United States of America,
EO-SEC-09-005 | including the Federal Reserve Act (12 U.S.C. § 221 et seq.), the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (15 U.S.C. § 78a et seq.),
EO-SEC-09-006 | the Bank Secrecy Act (31 U.S.C. § 5311 et seq.), and in order to promote financial stability, technological leadership,
EO-SEC-09-007 | and secure, non-intermediated capital formation, it is hereby ordered as follows:
EO-SEC-09-008 |
EO-SEC-09-009 | ### SECTION 1: STATEMENT OF POLICY AND FINDINGS
EO-SEC-09-010 | (a) The United States must maintain its leadership in global financial technology by fostering secure, resilient,
EO-SEC-09-011 | and decentralized software architectures. Traditional financial intermediation introduces systemic single points of
EO-SEC-09-012 | failure, increases transaction costs, and exposes consumer data to unnecessary third-party risks.
EO-SEC-09-013 | (b) Modern cryptographic protocols, automated gross-settlement vaults, and decentralized liquidity pools offer
EO-SEC-09-014 | mathematically verifiable alternatives to traditional intermediaries. It is the policy of the Federal Government
EO-SEC-09-015 | to protect the development of non-custodial software while establishing robust, automated mechanisms to interface
EO-SEC-09-016 | with national settlement systems.
EO-SEC-09-017 |
EO-SEC-09-018 | ### SECTION 2: DEFINITIONS
EO-SEC-09-019 | For the purposes of this order, the following definitions shall apply:
EO-SEC-09-020 | (a) "Non-Custodial Software" means any software, smart contract, or decentralized protocol that allows users to
EO-SEC-09-021 | retain sole cryptographic control over their private keys and digital assets without relying on a third-party custodian.
EO-SEC-09-022 | (b) "Automated Gross-Settlement Vault" (AGSV) means a decentralized, smart-contract-based liquidity pool designed
EO-SEC-09-023 | to execute real-time, atomic, and bilateral settlements of digital assets without an intermediary clearinghouse.
EO-SEC-09-024 | (c) "Dynamic Liquidity Sink" (DLS) means an algorithmic mechanism that dynamically adjusts liquidity reserves
EO-SEC-09-025 | within an AGSV to prevent systemic runs, stabilize transaction fees, and maintain peg-stability of digital assets.
EO-SEC-09-026 | (d) "Software Shield" refers to the legal and regulatory safe harbor established under Part 17 of this Order.
EO-SEC-09-027 |
EO-SEC-09-028 | ### SECTION 3: PART 17 - THE ANTI-INTERMEDIARY SOFTWARE SHIELD
EO-SEC-09-029 | (a) **Exemption from Broker-Dealer and Investment Adviser Status.**
EO-SEC-09-030 |   (i) No person or entity shall be deemed a "broker" or "dealer" under Section 3(a)(4) or 3(a)(5) of the Securities
EO-SEC-09-031 |   Exchange Act of 1934 (15 U.S.C. § 78c), nor an "investment adviser" under Section 202(a)(11) of the Investment
EO-SEC-09-032 |   Advisers Act of 1940, solely by virtue of writing, publishing, maintaining, or distributing non-custodial software.
EO-SEC-09-033 |   (ii) The writing of code is protected expression under the First Amendment of the United States Constitution.
EO-SEC-09-034 |   The Department of Justice, the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), and the Commodity Futures Trading
EO-SEC-09-035 |   Commission (CFTC) shall align their enforcement guidelines to respect this constitutional protection.
EO-SEC-09-036 | (b) **Clarification of Broker-Dealer Exemptions.**
EO-SEC-09-037 |   (i) The SEC shall update its guidance on Rule 15a-6 and related broker-dealer registration exemptions to explicitly
EO-SEC-09-038 |   exclude developers of decentralized exchange (DEX) smart contracts, provided that such developers do not hold,
EO-SEC-09-039 |   transfer, or exercise discretionary control over customer funds or execute transactions on behalf of specific users.
EO-SEC-09-040 |   (ii) The possession of administrative keys ("admin keys") or multi-signature governance rights for the sole purpose
EO-SEC-09-041 |   of protocol upgrades or emergency pauses shall not, by itself, constitute "custody" or "control" of customer assets,
EO-SEC-09-042 |   provided that such keys cannot be used to unilaterally misappropriate user funds.
EO-SEC-09-043 | (c) **Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Safe Harbor.**
EO-SEC-09-044 |   (i) The Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) shall issue revised guidance clarifying that developers,
EO-SEC-09-045 |   validators, miners, and node operators of decentralized networks are not "money transmitters" under 31 U.S.C. § 5312
EO-SEC-09-046 |   or 31 C.F.R. § 1010.100(ff), as they do not accept and transmit value as an intermediary business.
EO-SEC-09-047 |   (ii) Compliance with AML/CFT regulations shall be shifted from the protocol layer to the fiat-to-crypto on/off-ramps,
EO-SEC-09-048 |   preserving the permissionless nature of the underlying software infrastructure.
EO-SEC-09-049 |
EO-SEC-09-050 | ### SECTION 4: PART 18 - THE DYNAMIC LIQUIDITY SINK AND AUTOMATED GROSS-SETTLEMENT VAULTS
EO-SEC-09-051 | (a) **Establishment of Automated Gross-Settlement Vaults (AGSVs).**
EO-SEC-09-052 |   (i) The Federal Reserve Board, in coordination with the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC), shall
EO-SEC-09-053 |   establish a regulatory framework allowing chartered depository institutions to deploy and interact with AGSVs.
EO-SEC-09-054 |   (ii) AGSVs shall utilize smart contracts to execute real-time gross settlement (RTGS) of tokenized commercial bank
EO-SEC-09-055 |   deposits, central bank digital currencies (CBDCs), and qualified stablecoins, ensuring instant finality.
EO-SEC-09-056 | (b) **Dynamic Liquidity Sink (DLS) Architecture.**
EO-SEC-09-057 |   (i) To mitigate systemic liquidity shocks, AGSVs shall incorporate a Dynamic Liquidity Sink. The DLS shall
EO-SEC-09-058 |   algorithmically adjust reserve ratios, collateral haircuts, and transaction routing paths based on real-time
EO-SEC-09-059 |   on-chain volatility, liquidity density, and settlement velocity.
EO-SEC-09-060 |   (ii) The DLS shall be programmed to automatically absorb excess liquidity during periods of market contraction
EO-SEC-09-061 |   and release liquidity during periods of high transaction demand, acting as an algorithmic lender of last resort.
EO-SEC-09-062 | (c) **Integration with Federal Settlement Systems.**
EO-SEC-09-063 |   (i) The Federal Reserve shall develop secure Application Programming Interfaces (APIs) and smart contract bridges
EO-SEC-09-064 |   to connect AGSVs directly with the FedNow Service and the Fedwire Funds Service.
EO-SEC-09-065 |   (ii) This integration shall enable seamless, automated, and non-intermediated settlement between legacy fiat
EO-SEC-09-066 |   banking systems and decentralized cryptographic networks, reducing settlement risk to zero.
EO-SEC-09-067 |
EO-SEC-09-068 | ### SECTION 5: INTEROPERABILITY AND REGULATORY HARMONIZATION
EO-SEC-09-069 | (a) **Joint Task Force on Decentralized Settlement.**
EO-SEC-09-070 |   There is hereby established an Interagency Task Force on Decentralized Settlement, co-chaired by the Secretary of
EO-SEC-09-071 |   the Treasury, the Chairman of the Federal Reserve, the Chairman of the SEC, and the Chairman of the CFTC.
EO-SEC-09-072 | (b) **Mandate of the Task Force.**
EO-SEC-09-073 |   The Task Force shall, within 180 days of this order, publish a unified regulatory handbook detailing:
EO-SEC-09-074 |   (i) Standardized smart contract auditing guidelines for AGSVs to ensure code security and economic resilience.
EO-SEC-09-075 |   (ii) Interoperability protocols for cross-chain liquidity transfers under the Dynamic Liquidity Sink framework.
EO-SEC-09-076 |   (iii) Legal templates for "Software Shield" compliance, allowing developers to self-certify their non-custodial status.
EO-SEC-09-077 |
EO-SEC-09-078 | ### SECTION 6: IMPLEMENTATION AND REPORTING
EO-SEC-09-079 | (a) All executive departments and agencies shall take immediate steps, consistent with applicable law, to implement
EO-SEC-09-080 | the provisions of this order.
EO-SEC-09-081 | (b) The Secretary of the Treasury, in consultation with the Director of the Office of Management and Budget (OMB),
EO-SEC-09-082 | shall submit an annual report to the President on the economic impact of the Anti-Intermediary Software Shield
EO-SEC-09-083 | and the operational efficiency of the Dynamic Liquidity Sink.
