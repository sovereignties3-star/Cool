EO-SEC-07-001 # EXECUTIVE ORDER: ESTABLISHING THE NATIONAL QUANTUM-SECURED TRANSACTION ARCHITECTURE
EO-SEC-07-002 ## PART 13: LATENCY-EQUALIZED CLEARING CORRIDOR (LECC) AND ALGORITHMIC MITIGATION
EO-SEC-07-003 ### Section 13.1. Declaration of Systemic Vulnerability in High-Frequency Infrastructure
EO-SEC-07-004 The President of the United States finds that high-frequency algorithmic manipulation (HFAM) within
EO-SEC-07-005 national financial markets and critical data transmission networks poses an existential threat to
EO-SEC-07-006 economic stability, market integrity, and national security. Predatory latency arbitrage,
EO-SEC-07-007 front-running, and order-book manipulation executed by automated trading systems exploit physical
EO-SEC-07-008 geographic advantages and microsecond-level discrepancies in fiber-optic routing. To mitigate these
EO-SEC-07-009 vulnerabilities, there is hereby established the Latency-Equalized Clearing Corridor (LECC).
EO-SEC-07-010 ### Section 13.2. Mandate for Physical and Logical Fiber-Optic Equalization
EO-SEC-07-011 (a) The Federal Communications Commission (FCC), in coordination with the Department of Energy (DOE)
EO-SEC-07-012 and the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), shall oversee the deployment of
EO-SEC-07-013 physical fiber-optic corridors engineered to equalize propagation delays across all participating nodes.
EO-SEC-07-014 (b) All telecommunications carriers providing transit for financial clearing operations must implement
EO-SEC-07-015 physical delay lines, including coiled fiber spools and chromatic dispersion compensators, to ensure
EO-SEC-07-016 that the time-of-flight for data packets between any two nodes within the LECC is mathematically
EO-SEC-07-017 identical, regardless of the physical distance separating the nodes.
EO-SEC-07-018 (c) The target tolerance for latency equalization across the LECC shall not exceed five (5) nanoseconds,
EO-SEC-07-019 verified continuously via GPS-disciplined rubidium atomic clocks and optical interferometry.
EO-SEC-07-020 ### Section 13.3. Suppression of Algorithmic Manipulation and Arbitrage
EO-SEC-07-021 (a) Any entity executing transactions within the LECC is prohibited from utilizing predictive
EO-SEC-07-022 machine learning models designed to front-run retail or institutional order flows based on
EO-SEC-07-023 sub-millisecond network telemetry.
EO-SEC-07-024 (b) The Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC)
EO-SEC-07-025 shall deploy automated, real-time heuristic monitoring agents at each LECC ingress point to detect
EO-SEC-07-026 and neutralize spoofing, quote stuffing, and other high-frequency manipulative patterns.
EO-SEC-07-027 (c) Violations of LECC operational parameters shall result in immediate revocation of clearing privileges
EO-SEC-07-028 and the forfeiture of all assets associated with the manipulative algorithmic execution.
EO-SEC-07-029 ## PART 14: ABSOLUTE FINALITY CORE (AFC) AND NON-PROBABILISTIC CONSENSUS
EO-SEC-07-030 ### Section 14.1. Transition from Probabilistic to Deterministic State Finality
EO-SEC-07-031 The current reliance on probabilistic consensus mechanisms (such as Proof-of-Work or standard
EO-SEC-07-032 Proof-of-Stake) within distributed ledgers presents unacceptable risks of chain reorganizations,
EO-SEC-07-033 double-spend attacks, and temporary state forks. To guarantee absolute settlement finality,
EO-SEC-07-034 all federal clearing systems and critical financial market infrastructures (FMIs) shall transition
EO-SEC-07-035 to a non-probabilistic consensus model, designated as the Absolute Finality Core (AFC).
EO-SEC-07-036 ### Section 14.2. Technical Specifications of the Absolute Finality Core
EO-SEC-07-037 (a) The AFC shall utilize a mathematically proven Byzantine Fault Tolerant (BFT) consensus protocol
EO-SEC-07-038 that guarantees safety and liveness under a partial synchrony model, ensuring that once a block
EO-SEC-07-039 or transaction is committed, its state transition is immediately and absolutely immutable.
EO-SEC-07-040 (b) The consensus mechanism must reject any protocol that allows for forkable states or requires
EO-SEC-07-041 a "number of confirmations" to assume finality. Settlement must be achieved in a single,
EO-SEC-07-042 non-reversible step upon the signature of a supermajority (two-thirds plus one) of validated nodes.
EO-SEC-07-043 (c) The AFC validator set shall be geographically distributed across secure federal installations,
EO-SEC-07-044 hardened against electromagnetic pulse (EMP) attacks, and connected exclusively via the LECC.
EO-SEC-07-045 ### Section 14.3. Integration of LECC and AFC for Unified Transaction Security
EO-SEC-07-046 (a) The LECC and the AFC shall be integrated into a single, unified transaction processing layer.
EO-SEC-07-047 (b) The equalized latency of the LECC shall serve as the physical timing baseline for the AFC's
EO-SEC-07-048 consensus rounds, eliminating the possibility of Maximal Extractable Value (MEV) exploits,
EO-SEC-07-049 transaction reordering, or validator-level front-running.
EO-SEC-07-050 (c) All transaction proposals submitted to the AFC must be timestamped using the LECC's unified
EO-SEC-07-051 atomic clock network, ensuring absolute chronological ordering of state transitions.
EO-SEC-07-052 ### Section 14.4. Implementation Timeline and Interagency Oversight
EO-SEC-07-053 (a) Within one hundred and eighty (180) days of this Order, the Department of Homeland Security (DHS),
EO-SEC-07-054 in coordination with the Treasury Department and the Federal Reserve Board, shall publish the
EO-SEC-07-055 physical architecture designs and cryptographic specifications for the unified LECC-AFC system.
EO-SEC-07-056 (b) Full operational deployment of the LECC-AFC infrastructure across all designated systemically
EO-SEC-07-057 important financial institutions shall be completed no later than twenty-four (24) months
EO-SEC-07-058 from the date of this Order.
EO-SEC-07-059 (c) The National Security Agency (NSA) shall conduct continuous cryptographic audits of the AFC's
EO-SEC-07-060 non-probabilistic consensus algorithms to ensure resilience against quantum-computing-assisted
EO-SEC-07-061 decryption and adversarial state manipulation.
