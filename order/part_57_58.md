EO-SEC-29-001 # EXECUTIVE ORDER: NATIONAL SECURITY INTEGRATION AND DIGITAL RESILIENCY
EO-SEC-29-002 ## PART 57: LEGACY DOCUMENT OPTICAL BRIDGE (LDOB)
EO-SEC-29-003 ### Section 1. Purpose and Scope of the Legacy Document Optical Bridge
EO-SEC-29-004 This section establishes the Legacy Document Optical Bridge (LDOB) initiative, designed to modernize,
EO-SEC-29-005 secure, and ingest historical, non-digitized national security archives into active defense networks.
EO-SEC-29-006 The primary objective is to eliminate information silos caused by legacy physical media through the
EO-SEC-29-007 deployment of advanced Optical Character Recognition (OCR) scanning networks. These networks shall
EO-SEC-29-008 operate under strict cryptographic controls to prevent data leakage during the digitization process.
EO-SEC-29-009 
EO-SEC-29-010 ### Section 2. Technical Specifications for OCR Scanning Networks
EO-SEC-29-011 (a) All OCR scanning networks deployed under this order must utilize air-gapped hardware configurations
EO-SEC-29-012 to ensure that physical documents containing classified or sensitive compartmented information (SCI)
EO-SEC-29-013 are processed without exposure to public networks or unauthorized external systems.
EO-SEC-29-014 (b) The OCR engines must incorporate deep-learning neural networks trained on historical typography,
EO-SEC-29-015 degraded ink patterns, and multi-lingual military terminology to achieve a minimum accuracy threshold
EO-SEC-29-016 of 99.98% on documents dating from 1947 to the present.
EO-SEC-29-017 (c) Real-time cryptographic hashing must be applied to every digitized page image and its corresponding
EO-SEC-29-018 text output. This creates an immutable ledger entry, ensuring the integrity of the digital record
EO-SEC-29-019 against unauthorized modification or tampering during the ingestion pipeline.
EO-SEC-29-020 (d) Automated metadata tagging protocols shall extract classification markings, dates, authors, and
EO-SEC-29-021 operational codenames, mapping them directly to the Unified Defense Taxonomy (UDT).
EO-SEC-29-022 
EO-SEC-29-023 ### Section 3. Implementation and Interagency Coordination
EO-SEC-29-024 The Director of National Intelligence (DNI), in coordination with the Secretary of Defense, shall
EO-SEC-29-025 oversee the deployment of LDOB nodes across all major military archives, intelligence repositories,
EO-SEC-29-026 and federal records centers. Quarterly progress reports detailing ingestion volume, accuracy rates,
EO-SEC-29-027 and hardware integrity audits shall be submitted to the National Security Council (NSC).
EO-SEC-29-028 
EO-SEC-29-029 ## PART 58: NATIONAL SECURITY IDENTITY ANCHOR (NSIA)
EO-SEC-29-030 ### Section 1. Establishment of the National Security Identity Anchor
EO-SEC-29-031 To safeguard the integrity of tactical communications and distributed defense operations, there is
EO-SEC-29-032 hereby established the National Security Identity Anchor (NSIA) framework. This framework provides
EO-SEC-29-033 a decentralized, hardware-enforced identity verification system for all personnel, devices, and
EO-SEC-29-034 automated systems operating within the Department of Defense (DoD) information networks.
EO-SEC-29-035 
EO-SEC-29-036 ### Section 2. Deployment of National Defense Edge Routers
EO-SEC-29-037 (a) The NSIA shall be physically anchored within National Defense Edge Routers (NDERs) deployed at
EO-SEC-29-038 tactical forward operating bases, mobile command centers, and autonomous defense platforms.
EO-SEC-29-039 (b) NDERs must feature tamper-responsive Hardware Security Modules (HSMs) capable of zeroizing
EO-SEC-29-040 cryptographic keys within nanoseconds of detecting physical or electronic intrusion attempts.
EO-SEC-29-041 (c) Edge routers must execute continuous, zero-trust mutual authentication protocols for all connected
EO-SEC-29-042 tactical assets, utilizing post-quantum cryptographic algorithms to withstand advanced threats.
EO-SEC-29-043 (d) NDERs shall maintain localized, synchronized identity ledgers that allow full operational
EO-SEC-29-044 capability and secure peer-to-peer authentication even when completely disconnected from the
EO-SEC-29-045 centralized global command network (DODIN).
EO-SEC-29-046 
EO-SEC-29-047 ### Section 3. Integration of Legacy Optical Bridge and Identity Anchor
EO-SEC-29-048 (a) To ensure seamless security, all digitized records generated by the LDOB (Part 57) must be
EO-SEC-29-049 cryptographically signed and verified by an authorized NSIA edge router prior to ingestion into
EO-SEC-29-050 the secure defense cloud.
EO-SEC-29-051 (b) Access to the digitized legacy archives shall be strictly controlled via NSIA-enabled
EO-SEC-29-052 multi-factor biometric and cryptographic tokens, ensuring that only personnel with verified,
EO-SEC-29-053 real-time clearance profiles can retrieve historical intelligence.
EO-SEC-29-054 (c) Audit logs generated by both the LDOB scanning networks and NDER access points shall be
EO-SEC-29-055 cross-referenced automatically to detect anomalous access patterns or potential insider threats.
EO-SEC-29-056 
EO-SEC-29-057 ### Section 4. Compliance and Enforcement
EO-SEC-29-058 Non-compliance with the technical standards, cryptographic requirements, or physical security
EO-SEC-29-059 protocols outlined in Parts 57 and 58 of this Executive Order shall result in immediate
EO-SEC-29-060 suspension of network access privileges for the offending agency or command unit, pending a
EO-SEC-29-061 comprehensive security review by the Joint Cyber Command.
