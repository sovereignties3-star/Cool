EO-SEC-25-001 | # UNIFIED EXECUTIVE ORDER: PARTS 49 AND 50
EO-SEC-25-002 | 
EO-SEC-25-003 | ## SECTION 97: CONGRESSIONAL RECORD INSCRIPTION AND ARCHIVAL PRESERVATION
EO-SEC-25-004 | 
EO-SEC-25-005 | (a) **Archival Mandate and Congressional Recordation**
EO-SEC-25-006 | Pursuant to the Federal Register Act (44 U.S.C. § 1505) and the statutory framework governing the publication of the Congressional Record under 44 U.S.C. § 901 et seq., this Executive Order shall be formally transmitted to the Archivist of the United States and the Director of the Government Publishing Office (GPO) for permanent inscription. This action ensures that the legal, administrative, and historical record of this executive action is preserved within the official annals of the United States Government.
EO-SEC-25-007 | 
EO-SEC-25-008 | (b) **Library of Congress Integration and Historical Research**
EO-SEC-25-009 | The Librarian of Congress shall establish a dedicated, digitally preserved, and publicly accessible repository within the Library of Congress Archives to catalog this Order. This repository shall:
EO-SEC-25-010 | (1) Utilize metadata schemas compliant with the Dublin Core Metadata Initiative (DCMI) and the Encoded Archival Description (EAD) standards to ensure cross-platform interoperability and long-term digital preservation.
EO-SEC-25-011 | (2) Cross-reference the inscription with the United States Statutes at Large and the United States Code to trace the statutory delegations of authority cited herein.
EO-SEC-25-012 | (3) Incorporate historical precedents of executive-order preservation, referencing the historical transition of executive records from the Department of State to the National Archives and Records Administration (NARA) under the Federal Register Act of 1935.
EO-SEC-25-013 | 
EO-SEC-25-014 | (c) **Interagency Transmission Protocols**
EO-SEC-25-015 | Within twenty-four (24) hours of the signing of this Order, the Executive Clerk of the White House shall transmit certified electronic and physical copies of this instrument to the President of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of Representatives, requesting immediate reading and formal insertion into the daily proceedings of both chambers of Congress.

EO-SEC-25-017 | ## SECTION 98: UNILATERAL EXECUTIVE ENACTMENT PATH AND FALLBACK ROUTING
EO-SEC-25-018 | 
EO-SEC-25-019 | (a) **Declaration of Unilateral Enactment Authority**
EO-SEC-25-020 | In the event of a legislative impasse, congressional inaction, or procedural delay that threatens the timely execution of the critical national security and administrative directives contained within this Order, the President hereby asserts the full measure of executive authority vested by Article II of the Constitution of the United States, including but not limited to the Commander-in-Chief Clause and the Take Care Clause (Art. II, Sec. 3).
EO-SEC-25-021 | 
EO-SEC-25-022 | (b) **Establishment of Fallback Routing Tracks**
EO-SEC-25-023 | To guarantee uninterrupted implementation of this Order, a multi-tiered fallback routing system is hereby established. If any provision of this Order is blocked, stayed, or otherwise delayed by legislative or judicial action, the following routing tracks shall automatically activate:
EO-SEC-25-024 | (1) **Track Alpha (Administrative Reallocation)**: The Director of the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) shall immediately reallocate non-discretionary funds and administrative personnel to execute the core functions of the affected provisions, utilizing existing statutory transfer authorities under 31 U.S.C. § 1531 and 31 U.S.C. § 1534.
EO-SEC-25-025 | (2) **Track Beta (Emergency Declaration Integration)**: The Secretary of Homeland Security and the Secretary of Defense shall absorb the operational mandates of the blocked provisions under the authority of the National Emergencies Act (50 U.S.C. § 1601 et seq.) and the Stafford Act (42 U.S.C. § 5121 et seq.), reclassifying the directives as emergency response measures.
EO-SEC-25-026 | (3) **Track Gamma (Independent Agency Alignment)**: The President shall request that independent regulatory agencies, including but not limited to the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) and the Federal Trade Commission (FTC), utilize their independent rulemaking authorities under the Administrative Procedure Act (5 U.S.C. § 553) to mirror and enforce the substantive policies of this Order within their respective jurisdictions.
EO-SEC-25-027 | 
EO-SEC-25-028 | (c) **Office of Legal Counsel (OLC) and OIRA Review**
EO-SEC-25-029 | The Assistant Attorney General for the Office of Legal Counsel and the Administrator of the Office of Information and Regulatory Affairs (OIRA) shall continuously monitor the implementation of these fallback tracks. They shall issue expedited legal opinions and regulatory waivers to clear any administrative barriers, ensuring that the unilateral execution path remains legally insulated and operationally seamless.
EO-SEC-25-030 | 
EO-SEC-25-031 | (d) **Severability and Continuous Execution**
EO-SEC-25-032 | If any section, clause, or fallback track of this Order is held to be invalid by a court of competent jurisdiction, the remainder of this Order and the alternative fallback routing tracks shall not be affected thereby, and shall continue in full force and effect as if the invalid portion had been severed.
