EO-SEC-13-005 | ### PREAMBLE
EO-SEC-13-006 | By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and the laws of the United States of America,
EO-SEC-13-007 | including the Critical Infrastructure Defense Act, the Federal Information Security Modernization Act (FISMA),
EO-SEC-13-008 | and the Clarifying Lawful Oversight of Allocation and Regulatory Integrity of Technology Act (CLARITY Act),

EO-SEC-13-011 | ### SECTION 25: JULY 1ST SYNCHRONIZED LAUNCH KEY
EO-SEC-13-012 | (a) **Establishment of the Synchronized Launch Protocol.** To ensure the secure, resilient, and uninterrupted migration
EO-SEC-13-013 | of federal information systems and critical national infrastructure networks, there is hereby established the July 1st
EO-SEC-13-014 | Synchronized Launch Key (J1-SLK) protocol. This protocol dictates the precise cryptographic and operational alignment
EO-SEC-13-015 | required for transition from legacy architectures to next-generation decentralized and zero-trust frameworks.
EO-SEC-13-016 | 
EO-SEC-13-017 | (b) **Network Migration Synchronization.** All executive departments and agencies operating critical digital infrastructure
EO-SEC-13-018 | shall synchronize their migration schedules to align with the J1-SLK cryptographic key release.
EO-SEC-13-019 | (i) The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) and the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA)
EO-SEC-13-020 | shall jointly define the technical parameters, cryptographic algorithms, and validation vectors of the J1-SLK.
EO-SEC-13-021 | (ii) Migration phases must execute concurrently across all designated federal networks to prevent latency-induced security
EO-SEC-13-022 | vulnerabilities, routing loops, and state-desynchronization exploits during the transition window.
EO-SEC-13-023 | (iii) Legacy systems shall maintain read-only state replication during the transition window, which shall commence
EO-SEC-13-024 | precisely at 00:00:00 Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) on July 1st, and conclude upon successful key verification.
EO-SEC-13-025 | (iv) The Federal Chief Information Officer (CIO) shall oversee the real-time telemetry of the migration, maintaining
EO-SEC-13-026 | a unified dashboard of all agency transition states to ensure zero-drift synchronization across the enterprise.
EO-SEC-13-027 | 
EO-SEC-13-028 | (c) **Cryptographic Key Custody and Activation.** The physical and logical components of the Synchronized Launch Key
EO-SEC-13-029 | shall be distributed across three distinct federal entities to prevent single-point-of-failure vulnerabilities.
EO-SEC-13-030 | (i) The designated custodians shall be the Department of Defense (DoD), the Department of Homeland Security (DHS),
EO-SEC-13-031 | and the Department of Commerce (DoC).
EO-SEC-13-032 | (ii) Activation of the J1-SLK requires the cryptographic consensus of at least two of the three custodial entities,
EO-SEC-13-033 | executed via a secure multi-party computation (MPC) protocol.
EO-SEC-13-034 | (iii) In the event of a synchronization failure or detected anomaly, fallback protocols must immediately revert
EO-SEC-13-035 | networks to the pre-migration baseline established under Section 25(b)(iii) within a maximum allowable window of 180 seconds.

EO-SEC-13-037 | ### SECTION 26: SECTION 106 STRUCTURAL REWRITE
EO-SEC-13-038 | (a) **Amendment and Structural Rewrite.** Section 106 of Executive Order 14067 (relating to Ensuring Responsible
EO-SEC-13-039 | Development of Digital Assets) is hereby structurally rewritten and superseded in its entirety to conform with the
EO-SEC-13-040 | statutory mandates and definitions set forth in the CLARITY Act.
EO-SEC-13-041 | 
EO-SEC-13-042 | (b) **Non-Severable Legal Property Classes.** Under the authority of the CLARITY Act, digital assets, smart contracts,
EO-SEC-13-043 | and associated network infrastructure components are classified into distinct, non-severable legal property classes.
EO-SEC-13-044 | This classification system is designed to prevent jurisdictional fragmentation, ensure regulatory compliance, and protect
EO-SEC-13-045 | the structural integrity of decentralized networks.
EO-SEC-13-046 | (i) **Class I: Sovereign Cryptographic Assets.** These assets represent direct obligations of the federal government,
EO-SEC-13-047 | central bank digital currencies (CBDCs), or assets backed by sovereign reserves. They possess absolute legal immunity
EO-SEC-13-048 | and are legally non-severable from their underlying cryptographic ledgers and sovereign issuance protocols.
EO-SEC-13-049 | (ii) **Class II: Utility and Protocol Infrastructure Assets.** These assets comprise smart contracts, decentralized
EO-SEC-13-050 | consensus mechanisms, validation nodes, and native protocol tokens. They are legally bound to their operational networks
EO-SEC-13-051 | and cannot be severed, attached, or liquidated independently of the network's functional state or consensus rules.
EO-SEC-13-052 | (iii) **Class III: Hybrid Commercial and Tokenized Instruments.** These assets represent fractionalized real-world assets,
EO-SEC-13-053 | tokenized securities, or commercial paper. Their legal title is permanently anchored to the corresponding digital registry,
EO-SEC-13-054 | ensuring that any transfer of the digital token legally transfers the underlying physical or financial property, and vice versa.
EO-SEC-13-055 | 
EO-SEC-13-056 | (c) **Legal Non-Severability Doctrine.** To maintain systemic stability and prevent systemic risk within the financial sector,
EO-SEC-13-057 | any judicial, administrative, or private action attempting to sever a Class II or Class III asset from its native protocol,
EO-SEC-13-058 | consensus mechanism, or smart contract logic shall be deemed null and void under the CLARITY Act. Legal remedies for disputes
EO-SEC-13-059 | arising from these assets must be executed within the parameters of the protocol's state machine or through designated
EO-SEC-13-060 | federal arbitration channels.
EO-SEC-13-061 | 
EO-SEC-13-062 | (d) **Implementation and Enforcement.** The Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), the Commodity Futures Trading
EO-SEC-13-063 | Commission (CFTC), and the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) shall jointly promulgate rules within 90 days
EO-SEC-13-064 | of this Order to enforce these non-severable property classifications across all regulated entities and markets.
EO-SEC-13-065 | 
EO-SEC-13-066 | (e) **Interoperability Standards.** The Department of the Treasury, in coordination with the Federal Financial Institutions
EO-SEC-13-067 | Examination Council (FFIEC), shall establish interoperability standards to ensure that non-severable property classes
EO-SEC-13-068 | can be seamlessly integrated into existing banking and clearing systems without compromising regulatory oversight.
