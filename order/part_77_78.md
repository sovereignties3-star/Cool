EO-SEC-39-001 # EXECUTIVE ORDER: PART 77 & 78 UNIFIED FRAMEWORK
EO-SEC-39-002 
EO-SEC-39-003 ## SECTION 77: SWISS ALPINE INTERCEPT EXECUTION (BÜRGENSTOCK DELEGATIONS)
EO-SEC-39-004 
EO-SEC-39-005 ### Sub-Section 77.1: Establishment of the Alpine Security Corridor
EO-SEC-39-006 1. The Secretary of State, in coordination with the Secretary of Defense and the Director of National Intelligence, shall establish the Swiss Alpine Intercept Initiative (SAII).
EO-SEC-39-007 2. This initiative is designed to secure high-altitude transit corridors and coordinate intelligence sharing with the Swiss Federal Department of Foreign Affairs (FDFA).
EO-SEC-39-008 3. The primary operational focus shall be the monitoring and interception of unauthorized trans-alpine communications and physical transits.
EO-SEC-39-009 
EO-SEC-39-010 ### Sub-Section 77.2: Bürgenstock Delegation Coordination and Protocols
EO-SEC-39-011 1. Pursuant to the multilateral agreements established during the Bürgenstock Summit, the United States delegation shall maintain a permanent liaison office in Lucerne.
EO-SEC-39-012 2. This liaison office is tasked with executing the security mandates of the Bürgenstock Communiqué, specifically targeting nuclear safety and humanitarian transit.
EO-SEC-39-013 3. All intelligence intercepts executed within the Swiss Alpine sector shall be cross-referenced with the Bürgenstock delegation registry to prevent diplomatic friction.
EO-SEC-39-014 4. Joint tactical operations with Swiss security forces (Kantonspolizei Nidwalden) shall be conducted under strict adherence to Swiss sovereignty laws.
EO-SEC-39-015 
EO-SEC-39-016 ### Sub-Section 77.3: High-Altitude Surveillance and Interdiction Assets
EO-SEC-39-017 1. Deployment of advanced atmospheric and satellite-based surveillance assets over the Uri Alps and surrounding massifs is hereby authorized.
EO-SEC-39-018 2. Intercepted data shall be routed through the European Command (EUCOM) Joint Intelligence Operations Center for real-time analysis.
EO-SEC-39-019 3. Any physical interdiction of hostile actors within the designated alpine zone must be coordinated through the Swiss Federal Office of Police (fedpol).

EO-SEC-39-021 ## SECTION 78: 14-POINT ISLAMABAD MOU INTEGRATION (MARITIME TRANSIT AGREEMENTS)
EO-SEC-39-022 
EO-SEC-39-023 ### Sub-Section 78.1: Codification of the 14-Point Islamabad Memorandum of Understanding
EO-SEC-39-024 1. The United States formally integrates the 14-Point Islamabad MoU into its regional maritime security strategy for the Indian Ocean and Arabian Sea.
EO-SEC-39-025 2. The Department of State shall lead the bilateral commission to oversee the implementation of all fourteen points, focusing on mutual defense logistics.
EO-SEC-39-026 3. Key provisions regarding joint naval exercises, search and rescue operations, and maritime domain awareness are prioritized for immediate funding.
EO-SEC-39-027 
EO-SEC-39-028 ### Sub-Section 78.2: Maritime Transit and Port Security Protocols
EO-SEC-39-029 1. In alignment with Point 4 and Point 7 of the MoU, secure maritime transit corridors shall be established linking the Port of Karachi and Gwadar Port.
EO-SEC-39-030 2. The United States Coast Guard, in partnership with the Pakistan Maritime Security Agency (PMSA), shall conduct joint patrols to deter asymmetric threats.
EO-SEC-39-031 3. Automated customs harmonization systems shall be deployed at designated ports of entry to expedite verified commercial cargo while filtering contraband.
EO-SEC-39-032 
EO-SEC-39-033 ### Sub-Section 78.3: Regional Stability and Counter-Piracy Operations
EO-SEC-39-034 1. The Combined Maritime Forces (CMF) Task Force 150 shall serve as the primary multilateral vehicle for executing the counter-piracy mandates of the MoU.
EO-SEC-39-035 2. Intelligence sharing regarding illicit maritime traffic in the Gulf of Oman and the Arabian Sea shall be streamlined via the Islamabad Fusion Center.
EO-SEC-39-036 3. Annual reviews of the maritime transit agreements shall be submitted to the National Security Council to ensure alignment with Indo-Pacific strategy.

EO-SEC-39-038 ## SECTION 79: UNIFIED EXECUTION AND INTEROPERABILITY
EO-SEC-39-039 
EO-SEC-39-040 ### Sub-Section 79.1: Cross-Theater Intelligence Fusion
EO-SEC-39-041 1. The National Security Agency (NSA) shall establish a dedicated data bridge linking the Swiss Alpine Intercept network with the Islamabad Maritime Fusion Center.
EO-SEC-39-042 2. This unified data stream (Project Alpine-Ocean) shall utilize predictive AI models to identify trans-regional threat networks operating between Europe and South Asia.
EO-SEC-39-043 3. Quarterly operational reports detailing intercept metrics, diplomatic clearances, and maritime transit volumes shall be compiled for executive review.
