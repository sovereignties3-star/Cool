EO-SEC-33-001 # EXECUTIVE ORDER: SOVEREIGN DATA INTEGRITY AND DEMOCRATIC RESILIENCE
EO-SEC-33-002 
EO-SEC-33-003 ## PREAMBLE
EO-SEC-33-004 By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and the laws of the United States of America,
EO-SEC-33-005 including the National Security Act of 1947, the Help America Vote Act (HAVA), and the Cybersecurity Act of 2015,
EO-SEC-33-006 it is hereby ordered that the United States secure its critical intelligence pipelines and democratic infrastructure
EO-SEC-33-007 from foreign and domestic vulnerabilities inherent in commercial technology dependencies.
EO-SEC-33-008 
EO-SEC-33-009 ## PART 65: THE INTELLIGENCE DATABASE RE-ROUTE (IDR)
EO-SEC-33-010 ### Section 65.1: Findings and Declarations of Vulnerability
EO-SEC-33-011 (a) The integration of commercial cloud service providers (CSPs) within the Intelligence Community (IC)
EO-SEC-33-012 has introduced unacceptable systemic risks, including supply chain compromises, unauthorized data mining,
EO-SEC-33-013 and the potential for foreign adversary exploitation of commercial software vulnerabilities.
EO-SEC-33-014 (b) To safeguard national security, the United States must decouple its Tier-1 and Tier-2 intelligence databases
EO-SEC-33-015 from commercial tech servers and establish a fully sovereign, air-gapped, and government-controlled network.
EO-SEC-33-016 
EO-SEC-33-017 ### Section 65.2: Mandate for Commercial Decoupling and Re-Routing
EO-SEC-33-018 (a) Within 180 days of this Order, the Director of National Intelligence (DNI), in coordination with the
EO-SEC-33-019 Director of the Defense Information Systems Agency (DISA) and the Director of the National Security Agency (NSA),
EO-SEC-33-020 shall initiate the Intelligence Database Re-Route (IDR) protocol.
EO-SEC-33-021 (b) The IDR protocol shall systematically migrate all classified intelligence databases, including but not
EO-SEC-33-022 limited to those residing on commercial cloud infrastructures (e.g., Amazon Web Services Secret Region,
EO-SEC-33-023 Microsoft Azure Government, and Google Distributed Cloud Hosted), to the Sovereign Intelligence Cloud Initiative (SICI).
EO-SEC-33-024 (c) The SICI shall consist of physically isolated, government-owned, and government-operated data centers
EO-SEC-33-025 utilizing custom, open-source, audited hardware architectures free from commercial proprietary firmware.
EO-SEC-33-026 
EO-SEC-33-027 ### Section 65.3: Technical Specifications for the Sovereign Intelligence Cloud Initiative (SICI)
EO-SEC-33-028 (a) **Physical Air-Gapping**: All SICI nodes must be physically air-gapped from the public internet and
EO-SEC-33-029 commercial telecommunications networks. Data transit between nodes must occur via dedicated, military-grade
EO-SEC-33-030 fiber-optic networks utilizing continuous quantum key distribution (QKD) and post-quantum cryptography (PQC).
EO-SEC-33-031 (b) **Hardware Provenance**: All microprocessors, cryptographic modules, and storage controllers deployed
EO-SEC-33-032 within SICI must be manufactured within domestic, Trusted Foundry Program-certified facilities.
EO-SEC-33-033 (c) **Zero-Trust Architecture**: Access to the SICI shall be governed by a strict, continuous-authentication
EO-SEC-33-034 zero-trust architecture, utilizing multi-factor biometric verification and hardware-bound cryptographic keys.
EO-SEC-33-035 
EO-SEC-33-036 ### Section 65.4: Enforcement and Decommissioning of Commercial Nodes
EO-SEC-33-037 (a) Upon successful migration of an intelligence database to the SICI, all commercial hosting environments
EO-SEC-33-038 previously holding said data must undergo a certified, multi-pass cryptographic erasure (sanitization)
EO-SEC-33-039 in accordance with NIST Special Publication 800-88 Revision 1 (Guidelines for Media Sanitization).
EO-SEC-33-040 (b) The Inspector General of the Intelligence Community shall conduct bi-annual audits of all decommissioned
EO-SEC-33-041 commercial nodes to ensure zero residual data persistence of classified United States intelligence.
EO-SEC-33-042 
EO-SEC-33-043 ## PART 66: REAL-TIME VOTER ONBOARDING CORE (RTVOC)
EO-SEC-33-044 ### Section 66.1: Modernization of Democratic Infrastructure
EO-SEC-33-045 (a) The right to vote is a fundamental pillar of American democracy. To ensure the integrity, accessibility,
EO-SEC-33-046 and efficiency of the electoral process, the United States must transition from antiquated, fragmented,
EO-SEC-33-047 and paper-heavy voter registration systems to a secure, real-time, and automated onboarding framework.
EO-SEC-33-048 (b) This Part establishes the National Real-Time Voter Onboarding Core (RTVOC) to streamline and secure
EO-SEC-33-049 voter registration across all participating states and territories.
EO-SEC-33-050 
EO-SEC-33-051 ### Section 66.2: Establishment of the RTVOC Architecture
EO-SEC-33-052 (a) The Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA), in coordination with the Election Assistance
EO-SEC-33-053 Commission (EAC), shall design, deploy, and maintain the RTVOC technical architecture.
EO-SEC-33-054 (b) The RTVOC shall serve as a secure, decentralized verification engine that interfaces with state-level
EO-SEC-33-055 Departments of Motor Vehicles (DMVs), State Departments of Vital Statistics, and the Social Security Administration (SSA).
EO-SEC-33-056 (c) The RTVOC must be hosted on the secure, sovereign infrastructure established under Part 65 of this Order,
EO-SEC-33-057 completely isolated from commercial cloud environments and public-facing web servers.
EO-SEC-33-058 
EO-SEC-33-059 ### Section 66.3: Instant Voter Onboarding Protocols
EO-SEC-33-060 (a) **Automated Eligibility Trigger**: Upon an individual reaching the age of 18, or upon naturalization,
EO-SEC-33-061 the RTVOC shall automatically verify eligibility using cross-referenced records from the SSA and vital statistics.
EO-SEC-33-062 (b) **Zero-Knowledge Verification**: To protect citizen privacy, the RTVOC shall utilize Zero-Knowledge Proof (ZKP)
EO-SEC-33-063 cryptography. State election systems shall query the RTVOC to verify eligibility (e.g., "Is citizen X eligible?")
EO-SEC-33-064 and receive a cryptographically signed "Yes/No" response without transmitting or storing raw PII.
EO-SEC-33-065 (c) **Real-Time Synchronization**: State voter rolls must synchronize with the RTVOC in real-time. When a voter
EO-SEC-33-066 changes their primary residence (as verified by DMV or postal records), their registration must be updated
EO-SEC-33-067 instantaneously across jurisdictions, preventing dual-registration and ensuring accurate local voter rolls.
EO-SEC-33-068 
EO-SEC-33-069 ### Section 66.4: Security, Auditing, and Fail-Safe Mechanisms
EO-SEC-33-070 (a) **Immutable Ledger**: All voter onboarding transactions, modifications, and verifications processed
EO-SEC-33-071 by the RTVOC shall be recorded on a private, permissioned, and cryptographically signed immutable ledger.
EO-SEC-33-072 This ledger shall be auditable in real-time by authorized state and federal election officials.
EO-SEC-33-073 (b) **Paper-Trail Integration**: The RTVOC must generate a secure, physical paper-trail backup for every
EO-SEC-33-074 automated registration action. This backup must be printed and archived at the county or municipal level
EO-SEC-33-075 to serve as the ultimate source of truth in the event of a catastrophic system failure.
EO-SEC-33-076 (c) **Penetration Testing**: CISA shall conduct continuous, adversarial red-team testing of the RTVOC
EO-SEC-33-077 to identify and remediate potential vulnerabilities before they can be exploited by hostile actors.
EO-SEC-33-078 
EO-SEC-33-079 ## PART 67: INTEGRATION AND UNIFICATION OF SECURE PIPELINES
EO-SEC-33-080 ### Section 67.1: Unified Data Transit and Cross-Protocol Security
EO-SEC-33-081 (a) The intelligence database re-route (Part 65) and the voter onboarding core (Part 66) shall share a unified
EO-SEC-33-082 security architecture, leveraging the same sovereign, air-gapped hardware nodes to minimize overhead.
EO-SEC-33-083 (b) Strict logical separation (network slicing) must be maintained between the intelligence data pipelines
EO-SEC-33-084 and the voter onboarding verification pipelines to prevent any cross-contamination of classified intelligence
EO-SEC-33-085 and domestic voter registration data.
EO-SEC-33-086 (c) The Department of Homeland Security (DHS) and the Department of Defense (DoD) shall jointly oversee
EO-SEC-33-087 the deployment of these unified pipelines, ensuring that national security and democratic integrity are
EO-SEC-33-088 treated as interdependent pillars of the nation's sovereign defense posture.
EO-SEC-33-089 
EO-SEC-33-090 ### Section 67.2: Implementation Timeline and Reporting
EO-SEC-33-091 (a) Within 30 days of this Order, a Joint Task Force on Sovereign Data Infrastructure (JTF-SDI) shall be
EO-SEC-33-092 established, co-chaired by the DNI, the Director of CISA, and the Secretary of Defense.
EO-SEC-33-093 (b) The JTF-SDI shall submit a comprehensive implementation plan to the President within 90 days, detailing
EO-SEC-33-094 the exact budgetary requirements, hardware acquisition timelines, and state-level integration protocols.
EO-SEC-33-095 (c) Quarterly progress reports shall be submitted to the Congressional Joint Committee on National Security
EO-SEC-33-096 and the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence to ensure transparency and rigorous oversight.
