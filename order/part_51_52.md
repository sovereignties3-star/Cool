EO-SEC-26-005 SUBCHAPTER XXVI - CONTINUITY OF CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE AND SURVEILLANCE OVERSIGHT

EO-SEC-26-007 PART 51: POST-LAPSE GOVERNANCE FILTER (VOTER-IDENTITY VALIDATION AND SAVE ACT CONTINUITY)
EO-SEC-26-008 
EO-SEC-26-009 Section 51.1. Establishment of the Post-Lapse Governance Filter.
EO-SEC-26-010 In the event of a lapse in federal appropriations, legislative authority, or statutory
EO-SEC-26-011 reauthorization, there is hereby established a Post-Lapse Governance Filter (PLGF).
EO-SEC-26-012 The PLGF shall serve as an administrative and operational mechanism to ensure the
EO-SEC-26-013 uninterrupted execution of critical national security, public safety, and constitutional
EO-SEC-26-014 integrity protocols. The PLGF shall prevent the degradation of essential federal-to-state
EO-SEC-26-015 data verification pipelines, specifically prioritizing voter-identity validation systems.
EO-SEC-26-016 
EO-SEC-26-017 Section 51.2. Integration of SAVE America Act Voter-Identity Validation Blocks.
EO-SEC-26-018 (a) Pursuant to the principles of the Safeguarding American Voter Eligibility (SAVE) Act,
EO-SEC-26-019 the Department of Homeland Security (DHS), in coordination with the Social Security
EO-SEC-26-020 Administration (SSA) and state election authorities, shall maintain real-time,
EO-SEC-26-021 high-availability access to the Systematic Alien Verification for Entitlements (SAVE)
EO-SEC-26-022 database. This access must remain fully operational during any federal funding lapse.
EO-SEC-26-023 (b) State election officials shall be provided with uninterrupted, automated validation
EO-SEC-26-024 blocks to verify the citizenship status of individuals registering to vote in federal
EO-SEC-26-025 elections. If a federal lapse occurs, the DHS Chief Information Officer shall implement
EO-SEC-26-026 the "Governance Filter Protocol," which reallocates non-lapsed emergency operational funds
EO-SEC-26-027 to prevent any downtime in the citizenship verification API endpoints.
EO-SEC-26-028 (c) Any attempt to suspend, throttle, or disable the SAVE validation blocks during a
EO-SEC-26-029 lapse period by any federal agency or personnel shall be deemed a violation of this Order,
EO-SEC-26-030 triggering immediate administrative review and potential reallocation of agency resources.
EO-SEC-26-031 
EO-SEC-26-032 Section 51.3. State-Level Registry Safeguards and Non-Citizen Database Cross-Referencing.
EO-SEC-26-033 (a) To prevent the dilution of lawful votes, the PLGF mandates that the DHS and the
EO-SEC-26-034 Department of Justice (DOJ) establish a decentralized, encrypted mirror of the active
EO-SEC-26-035 citizenship verification registry. This mirror shall be accessible to certified state
EO-SEC-26-036 election authorities under strict multi-factor authentication protocols.
EO-SEC-26-037 (b) In the event of a total network partition or federal database offline status, states
EO-SEC-26-038 are authorized to utilize the last-known synchronized local cryptographic ledger of
EO-SEC-26-039 citizenship status to perform provisional voter-identity validation, ensuring that no
EO-SEC-26-040 non-citizen registration is finalized during the lapse window.
EO-SEC-26-041 
EO-SEC-26-042 PART 52: FISC EXTENSION HARD-LOCK (FISA SECTION 702 LAPSE MITIGATION)
EO-SEC-26-043 
EO-SEC-26-044 Section 52.1. Mitigation of Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act (FISA) Section 702 Lapses.
EO-SEC-26-045 (a) This Section addresses the critical national security vulnerabilities arising from a
EO-SEC-26-046 statutory lapse of Section 702 of the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act (FISA).
EO-SEC-26-047 (b) Upon the expiration of Section 702 authority without immediate congressional
EO-SEC-26-048 reauthorization, the Director of National Intelligence (DNI) and the Attorney General
EO-SEC-26-049 shall immediately activate the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Court (FISC) Extension
EO-SEC-26-050 Hard-Lock protocol.
EO-SEC-26-051 
EO-SEC-26-052 Section 52.2. The FISC Extension Hard-Lock Protocol.
EO-SEC-26-053 (a) The "Hard-Lock" is defined as an automated, legally binding operational state that
EO-SEC-26-054 freezes all active surveillance targets under existing FISC certifications at the exact
EO-SEC-26-055 moment of statutory lapse. No new target acquisitions, selectors, or upstream/downstream
EO-SEC-26-056 collection parameters may be initiated or modified during the lapse period.
EO-SEC-26-057 (b) To prevent a catastrophic intelligence blind spot, existing, court-approved
EO-SEC-26-058 certifications targeting non-U.S. persons reasonably believed to be located outside the
EO-SEC-26-059 United States shall be legally extended under the inherent constitutional authority of the
EO-SEC-26-060 Executive Branch to protect the nation from imminent foreign threats, subject to immediate
EO-SEC-26-061 and continuous ex parte review by the FISC.
EO-SEC-26-062 (c) The Hard-Lock strictly prohibits the collection of any communications where the target
EO-SEC-26-063 or any party to the communication is a known United States person, unless a specific,
EO-SEC-26-064 individualized warrant has been issued under Title I or Title III of FISA.
EO-SEC-26-065 
EO-SEC-26-066 Section 52.3. Civil Liberties Safeguards, Minimization, and Auditing.
EO-SEC-26-067 (a) During a Section 702 lapse and the subsequent activation of the FISC Hard-Lock, the
EO-SEC-26-068 DOJ Office of Privacy and Civil Liberties (OPCL) and the ODNI Civil Liberties Protection
EO-SEC-26-069 Officer (CLPO) shall conduct daily audits of all data ingested under the extended
EO-SEC-26-070 certifications.
EO-SEC-26-071 (b) Any data acquired during the lapse period that is found to contain U.S. person
EO-SEC-26-072 identifiers or domestic communications must be immediately purged ("hard-deleted") from
EO-SEC-26-073 all intelligence community databases within twenty-four (24) hours of discovery.
EO-SEC-26-074 (c) A comprehensive, unclassified report detailing all collection activities, minimization
EO-SEC-26-075 actions, and compliance incidents during the lapse window shall be submitted to the
EO-SEC-26-076 House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence (HPSCI), the Senate Select Committee
EO-SEC-26-077 on Intelligence (SSCI), and the FISC within fifteen (15) days of authority restoration.
EO-SEC-26-078 
EO-SEC-26-079 Section 52.4. Interoperability of the Governance Filter and the FISC Hard-Lock.
EO-SEC-26-080 The PLGF established in Part 51 and the FISC Hard-Lock established in Part 52 shall operate
EO-SEC-26-081 in tandem. The National Security Council (NSC) shall oversee the integration of these
EO-SEC-26-082 systems, ensuring that federal identity verification databases (SAVE Act) and foreign
EO-SEC-26-083 intelligence collection systems (FISA) maintain strict operational separation while
EO-SEC-26-084 simultaneously executing their respective continuity protocols to safeguard the homeland.
