EO-SEC-08-001 # EXECUTIVE ORDER: SECTION 15 & 16
EO-SEC-08-002 ## PART 15: SANDBOX ERADICATION MANDATE (RUNTIME INTEGRITY)
EO-SEC-08-003 ### SUBSECTION 15.1: ELIMINATION OF UNAUTHORIZED VIRTUALIZED ENVIRONMENTS
EO-SEC-08-004 This directive mandates the immediate identification, containment, and eradication of all non-sanctioned, isolated testing environments,
EO-SEC-08-005 hereafter referred to as "unauthorized sandboxes," across all federal, military, and critical infrastructure networks.
EO-SEC-08-006 Unauthorized sandboxes present an unacceptable vector for covert malware development, evasion analysis, and data exfiltration by adversarial actors.
EO-SEC-08-007 All agencies shall deploy automated discovery protocols to map all hypervisors, container runtimes, and nested virtualization layers.
EO-SEC-08-008 ### SUBSECTION 15.2: IMPLEMENTATION OF GROSS EXECUTION SWITCHES
EO-SEC-08-009 To enforce this mandate, all hardware platforms hosting sovereign systems must implement firmware-level "Gross Execution Switches" (GES).
EO-SEC-08-010 A Gross Execution Switch is defined as a hardware-enforced or low-level kernel-level mechanism capable of instantly terminating execution
EO-SEC-08-011 of any unauthorized hypervisor or virtual machine monitor (VMM).
EO-SEC-08-012 Upon detection of an unaligned sandbox environment, the GES shall trigger an immediate, non-bypassable hardware halt, purging volatile memory
EO-SEC-08-013 and locking the physical host.
EO-SEC-08-014 The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) shall define the technical specifications for GES integration within ninety (90) days.
EO-SEC-08-015 ## PART 16: SOVEREIGN MASTER REVOCATION KEY (CRYPTOGRAPHIC SOVEREIGNTY)
EO-SEC-08-016 ### SUBSECTION 16.1: ESTABLISHMENT OF THE SOVEREIGN MASTER REVOCATION KEY
EO-SEC-08-017 There is hereby established the Sovereign Master Revocation Key (SMRK), a singular, supreme cryptographic root-of-trust.
EO-SEC-08-018 The SMRK shall serve as the ultimate authority to invalidate, revoke, or nullify any digital signature, cryptographic certificate,
EO-SEC-08-019 or identity credential within the sovereign digital domain.
EO-SEC-08-020 The SMRK shall be generated using state-of-the-art post-quantum cryptographic algorithms, specifically adhering to NIST-approved lattice-based standards.
EO-SEC-08-021 ### SUBSECTION 16.2: MASTER CRYPTOGRAPHIC CERTIFICATES AND HARDWARE SECURITY MODULES
EO-SEC-08-022 The SMRK shall be instantiated across a distributed array of ultra-secure, air-gapped Hardware Security Modules (HSMs) utilizing multi-party computation.
EO-SEC-08-023 No single entity, agency, or individual shall possess the capability to activate the SMRK independently; activation requires a threshold consensus.
EO-SEC-08-024 All subordinate master cryptographic certificates issued by federal agencies must contain an embedded, immutable revocation hook tied directly to the SMRK.
EO-SEC-08-025 Any certificate found lacking this hook shall be deemed untrusted and automatically blocked by the unified operating system kernel.
EO-SEC-08-026 ### SUBSECTION 16.3: INTEROPERABILITY AND ENFORCEMENT
EO-SEC-08-027 The Department of Homeland Security, in coordination with the Department of Defense, shall oversee the deployment of the SMRK across all sectors.
EO-SEC-08-028 All legacy cryptographic systems must be migrated to support SMRK-compatible master cryptographic certificates within one hundred and eighty (180) days.
EO-SEC-08-029 Failure to comply with the integration timeline shall result in the immediate revocation of the non-compliant entity's digital operating authority.
