EO-SEC-19-001 # EXECUTIVE ORDER: DIGITAL SOVEREIGNTY AND ALGORITHMIC FREEDOM
EO-SEC-19-002 ## PART 37: THE SELF-CUSTODY CONSTITUTIONAL GUARANTEE
EO-SEC-19-003 ### Section 3701: Declaration of Policy and Constitutional Foundations
EO-SEC-19-004 (a) The right of the people to be secure in their persons, houses, papers, and effects, against unreasonable searches and
EO-SEC-19-005 seizures, shall not be violated. In the modern digital era, "papers and effects" must be interpreted to include private
EO-SEC-19-006 cryptographic keys, decentralized identifiers, personal data stores, and self-hosted digital wallets.
EO-SEC-19-007 (b) A private key is a mathematical instrument of personal privacy, equivalent to a physical lock and key protecting a
EO-SEC-19-008 private dwelling. Consequently, any government mandate requiring the disclosure of private keys, or the forced use of
EO-SEC-19-009 custodial intermediaries, constitutes an unreasonable search and seizure under the Fourth Amendment, absent a specific,
EO-SEC-19-010 individualized warrant based on probable cause and issued by a court of competent jurisdiction.
EO-SEC-19-011 (c) Furthermore, this Order recognizes an extension of Third Amendment protections to the digital domain. Just as the
EO-SEC-19-012 Third Amendment prohibits the quartering of soldiers in private homes without consent, it similarly prohibits the
EO-SEC-19-013 forced insertion of government-mandated monitoring software, backdoors, or state-sponsored surveillance nodes within
EO-SEC-19-014 an individual's private digital infrastructure, personal servers, or self-custodied hardware wallets.
EO-SEC-19-015 ### Section 3702: Prohibition of Custodial Mandates and Intermediary Coercion
EO-SEC-19-016 (a) No federal agency, department, or instrumentality shall promulgate rules, regulations, or guidance that penalize,
EO-SEC-19-017 restrict, or disincentivize individuals from maintaining self-custody of their digital assets, sovereign identities,
EO-SEC-19-018 or personal data. Any regulatory framework attempting to outlaw peer-to-peer transactions or self-hosted wallets is
EO-SEC-19-019 hereby declared null and void.
EO-SEC-19-020 (b) Citizens retain the unalienable right to transact directly with one another using cryptographic proofs without
EO-SEC-19-021 intermediary oversight. The Department of the Treasury and the Securities and Exchange Commission shall align their
EO-SEC-19-022 regulatory frameworks to respect the non-custodial nature of decentralized protocols, ensuring that software developers
EO-SEC-19-023 and non-custodial service providers are not classified as financial institutions or money transmitters.
EO-SEC-19-024 ## PART 38: THE OPEN-SOURCE AUTHOR CIVIL RIGHTS ACT
EO-SEC-19-025 ### Section 3801: Protection of Mathematical Logic and Algorithmic Expression
EO-SEC-19-026 (a) This Section establishes robust civil rights protections for authors, developers, and publishers of open-source software.
EO-SEC-19-027 Computer code, mathematical algorithms, and cryptographic protocols are recognized as pure speech and expressive activity
EO-SEC-19-028 protected under the First Amendment of the United States Constitution, consistent with established judicial precedents
EO-SEC-19-029 including Bernstein v. United States Department of Justice.
EO-SEC-19-030 (b) The publication of mathematical logic, including smart contract code, zero-knowledge proof protocols, and decentralized
EO-SEC-19-031 software, is a protected form of academic, scientific, and political expression. No developer shall be held criminally
EO-SEC-19-032 or civilly liable solely for the act of writing, publishing, or distributing open-source code.
EO-SEC-19-033 (c) Liability shall only attach upon clear and convincing evidence of direct, intentional participation in a specific
EO-SEC-19-034 criminal conspiracy, distinct from the mere creation, maintenance, or publication of neutral, dual-use software tools.
EO-SEC-19-035 The state shall not prosecute the architect of a tool for the subsequent misuse of that tool by third parties.
EO-SEC-19-036 ### Section 3802: Enforcement, Civil Remedies, and Administrative Accountability
EO-SEC-19-037 (a) Any federal official or agency that attempts to suppress, censor, or prosecute an open-source developer for the
EO-SEC-19-038 publication of mathematical logic or code shall be subject to immediate administrative review and potential civil liability.
EO-SEC-19-039 (b) The Department of Justice shall establish a Civil Rights Task Force to monitor and defend open-source developers
EO-SEC-19-040 against regulatory overreach and unconstitutional speech restrictions. This Task Force shall provide legal support and
EO-SEC-19-041 intervene in actions where federal agencies attempt to enforce sanctions or penalties against non-custodial software authors.
EO-SEC-19-042 (c) This Order ensures that the United States remains the global leader in technological innovation by securing the
EO-SEC-19-043 fundamental liberties of its digital architects and mathematical pioneers, fostering an environment of open scientific
EO-SEC-19-044 inquiry and cryptographic resilience.
