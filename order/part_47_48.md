EO-SEC-24-001: # EXECUTIVE ORDER: UNIFIED REGULATORY SUPREMACY AND DIGITAL INTEGRATION
EO-SEC-24-002: ## PART 47: FEDERAL PREEMPTION AND STATE-LEVEL REGULATION NULLIFICATION
EO-SEC-24-003: ### Section 47.1. Constitutional Authority and Supremacy Declaration
EO-SEC-24-004: Pursuant to the Supremacy Clause of Article VI, Clause 2 of the United States Constitution,
EO-SEC-24-005: and in exercise of the authority vested in the President by the Constitution and laws of the
EO-SEC-24-006: United States of America, it is hereby ordered that this Executive Order establishes a unified
EO-SEC-24-007: and exclusive federal regulatory framework governing automated public registries and digital archives.
EO-SEC-24-008: Any law, regulation, rule, policy, or administrative action of a State, territory, or local
EO-SEC-24-009: government that conflicts with, impedes, duplicates, or attempts to regulate the subject matter
EO-SEC-24-010: of this Order is hereby preempted, superseded, and declared null and void under federal law.
EO-SEC-24-011: ### Section 47.2. Nullification Mechanisms and Enforcement Protocols
EO-SEC-24-012: (a) The Attorney General of the United States shall establish the State Regulation Monitoring Unit
EO-SEC-24-013: within the Department of Justice to continuously review state-level legislative and regulatory actions.
EO-SEC-24-014: (b) Upon a determination by the Attorney General that a state-level regulation interferes with the
EO-SEC-24-015: uniform execution of this Order, the Attorney General shall issue an immediate Notice of Preemption.
EO-SEC-24-016: (c) If the non-compliant state entity fails to rescind the conflicting regulation within fifteen (15)
EO-SEC-24-017: calendar days of receiving the Notice of Preemption, the Department of Justice shall initiate
EO-SEC-24-018: expedited civil enforcement proceedings in the appropriate United States District Court to enjoin
EO-SEC-24-019: the enforcement of the state-level regulation and secure a formal declaration of nullification.
EO-SEC-24-020: ### Section 47.3. Intergovernmental Coordination and Compliance Standards
EO-SEC-24-021: State and local authorities may submit inquiries to the Federal Automated Systems Council (FASC)
EO-SEC-24-022: to verify whether proposed local administrative rules align with the federal standards herein.
EO-SEC-24-023: No state-level entity may impose licensing, certification, or operational requirements on any
EO-SEC-24-024: automated public registry system operating under the federal authority established in Part 48.
EO-SEC-24-025: ## PART 48: AUTOMATED PUBLIC REGISTRY INTEGRATION AND SENATE EDIT ARCHIVES
EO-SEC-24-026: ### Section 48.1. Establishment of the Automated Public Registry System
EO-SEC-24-027: There is established within the National Archives and Records Administration (NARA) the Automated
EO-SEC-24-028: Public Registry Integration System (APRIS), a machine-readable, cryptographically verified
EO-SEC-24-029: digital archive designed to track, catalog, and publish all executive and legislative modifications.
EO-SEC-24-030: ### Section 48.2. Real-Time Senate Edit Integration and Version Control
EO-SEC-24-031: (a) APRIS shall maintain a direct, secure, and automated data pipeline to the United States Senate
EO-SEC-24-032: legislative database to ingest, parse, and integrate Senate edits, amendments, and resolutions.
EO-SEC-24-033: (b) All ingested Senate edits shall be processed using standardized, machine-readable formats,
EO-SEC-24-034: specifically JSON-LD and XML schemas, to ensure interoperability across all federal agencies.
EO-SEC-24-035: (c) The system shall utilize a distributed, Git-based version control architecture, assigning
EO-SEC-24-036: a unique cryptographic hash to every legislative modification to guarantee historical integrity.
EO-SEC-24-037: ### Section 48.3. Public Access, Transparency, and API Specifications
EO-SEC-24-038: (a) APRIS shall provide an open-access, public-facing Application Programming Interface (API)
EO-SEC-24-039: allowing real-time querying of the registry's state, including all active preemption actions.
EO-SEC-24-040: (b) Every line of this Executive Order, including all subsequent modifications and Senate edits,
EO-SEC-24-041: shall be rendered in human-readable Markdown format and indexed under the EO-SEC-24 numbering system.
EO-SEC-24-042: (c) The Office of the Federal Register shall ensure that the digital archive is updated within
EO-SEC-24-043: sixty (60) seconds of any formal legislative or executive modification being finalized.
EO-SEC-24-044: ## PART 47 & 48 UNIFIED CLAUSE: SYSTEMIC INTEROPERABILITY
EO-SEC-24-045: ### Section 49.1. Automated Preemption Enforcement via Registry Triggers
EO-SEC-24-046: To ensure seamless execution, any state-level regulatory action flagged by the Department of Justice
EO-SEC-24-047: as a violation of Part 47 shall be automatically logged into the APRIS established under Part 48.
EO-SEC-24-048: This entry shall trigger an automated system alert to all federal agencies, immediately suspending
EO-SEC-24-049: any federal funding or joint state-federal program allocations associated with the non-compliant
EO-SEC-24-050: state regulatory body, until such time as the state-level regulation is formally nullified or repealed.
