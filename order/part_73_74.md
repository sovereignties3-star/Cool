EO-SEC-37-001 # EXECUTIVE ORDER: SECURE INTER-AGENCY COMMUNICATIONS AND DATA SOVEREIGNTY ENFORCEMENT
EO-SEC-37-002 ## PART 73: SECURE INTER-AGENCY COMMUNICATIONS CORE (AETHEL CORE INTEGRATION)
EO-SEC-37-003 ### Section 7301: Establishment of the Closed-Loop Aethel Core Network Architecture
EO-SEC-37-004 (a) There is hereby established the Closed-Loop Aethel Core Network (CLACN), a quantum-resistant,
EO-SEC-37-005 air-gapped communication infrastructure designed to serve as the primary medium for all high-level
EO-SEC-37-006 inter-agency intelligence sharing, diplomatic cables, and national security decision-making processes.
EO-SEC-37-007 (b) The CLACN shall operate independently of the public internet and standard federal wide-area networks,
EO-SEC-37-008 utilizing dedicated fiber-optic arrays and localized quantum key distribution (QKD) nodes.
EO-SEC-37-009 (c) The core cryptographic engine of this network shall be the Aethel Core, a proprietary decentralized
EO-SEC-37-010 ledger and cryptographic processing unit capable of real-time threat detection and self-healing routing.
EO-SEC-37-011 ### Section 7302: Mandatory Migration and Interoperability Standards
EO-SEC-37-012 (a) All executive departments, agencies, and associated contractors handling classified national security
EO-SEC-37-013 information shall migrate their primary communication channels to the CLACN within 180 days of this Order.
EO-SEC-37-014 (b) The Director of the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA), in coordination with the
EO-SEC-37-015 Director of National Intelligence (DNI), shall oversee the deployment of Aethel Core hardware modules
EO-SEC-37-016 to all designated federal facilities, ensuring physical security perimeters are established around all nodes.
EO-SEC-37-017 (c) Legacy systems shall be interfaced with the CLACN only through secure, unidirectional gateways (data diodes)
EO-SEC-37-018 approved by the National Security Agency (NSA) to prevent reverse-channel exploitation or data leakage.
EO-SEC-37-019 ### Section 7303: Operational Security and Zero-Trust Protocols
EO-SEC-37-020 (a) Access to the CLACN shall be governed by a strict zero-trust architecture, requiring continuous
EO-SEC-37-021 cryptographic authentication, biometric verification, and multi-party authorization for data access.
EO-SEC-37-022 (b) Any unauthorized attempt to access, probe, or map the CLACN topology shall be treated as a major national
EO-SEC-37-023 security incident and referred immediately to the Department of Justice and the Federal Bureau of Investigation.
EO-SEC-37-024 ## PART 74: DATA SOVEREIGNTY ENFORCEMENT PROTOCOL (ASSET FREEZE MANDATES)
EO-SEC-37-025 ### Section 7401: Definition of Sovereign Data Assets and Jurisdictional Boundaries
EO-SEC-37-026 (a) Sovereign Data Assets are defined as any digital information, metadata, cryptographic keys, or algorithmic
EO-SEC-37-027 models generated by, owned by, or held on behalf of the Federal Government, its citizens, or domestic entities
EO-SEC-37-028 whose compromise poses a direct threat to the economic, social, or physical security of the United States.
EO-SEC-37-029 (b) All Sovereign Data Assets must reside physically and logically within the territorial boundaries of the
EO-SEC-37-030 United States, subject exclusively to United States law and the regulatory oversight of federal authorities.
EO-SEC-37-031 ### Section 7402: Enforcement Mechanisms and Asset Freeze Penalties
EO-SEC-37-032 (a) The Secretary of the Treasury, in consultation with the Secretary of Commerce and the Attorney General,
EO-SEC-37-033 shall impose blocking sanctions and freeze all assets, property, and interests in property of any foreign
EO-SEC-37-034 or domestic individual, corporation, or entity found to have violated the integrity of United States data sovereignty.
EO-SEC-37-035 (b) Violations triggering immediate asset freezes under this Section shall include, but are not limited to:
EO-SEC-37-036 (1) Unauthorized exfiltration, transfer, or hosting of Sovereign Data Assets outside United States jurisdiction;
EO-SEC-37-037 (2) Facilitating foreign state access to domestic communication infrastructure or cloud storage repositories;
EO-SEC-37-038 (3) Failure to comply with federal data localization directives or Aethel Core integration mandates.
EO-SEC-37-039 (c) Any financial institution operating within the United States that fails to freeze accounts associated with
EO-SEC-37-040 sanctioned entities under this Protocol shall be subject to immediate charter revocation and civil penalties.
EO-SEC-37-041 ### Section 7403: Inter-Agency Task Force on Data Sovereignty
EO-SEC-37-042 (a) There is established an Inter-Agency Task Force on Data Sovereignty (ITFDS), chaired by the Secretary of
EO-SEC-37-043 Homeland Security, to monitor global data flows and identify potential vulnerabilities in domestic infrastructure.
EO-SEC-37-044 (b) The ITFDS shall submit quarterly classified reports to the President detailing foreign intelligence threats
EO-SEC-37-045 targeting the CLACN and recommending specific entities for inclusion on the Department of the Treasury's
EO-SEC-37-046 Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons List (SDN List).
EO-SEC-37-047 ## PART 75: IMPLEMENTATION AND SEVERABILITY
EO-SEC-37-048 ### Section 7501: Funding and Resource Allocation
EO-SEC-37-049 (a) Executive departments and agencies shall prioritize the allocation of existing budgetary resources to
EO-SEC-37-050 satisfy the requirements of Part 73 and Part 74 of this Order, supplemented by emergency national security funds.
EO-SEC-37-051 ### Section 7502: Severability Clause
EO-SEC-37-052 (a) If any provision of this Order, or the application of any provision to any person or circumstance, is held
EO-SEC-37-053 to be invalid, the remainder of this Order and the application of its provisions to other persons or circumstances
EO-SEC-37-054 shall not be affected thereby and shall remain in full force and effect.
