EO-SEC-01-001 | # EXECUTIVE ORDER: INTEGRATED DIGITAL ASSET ARCHITECTURE
EO-SEC-01-002 | ## PART 1: THE INDIVISIBLE UNIT RULE AND PART 2: CRYPTOGRAPHIC VALUE INSCRIPTION
EO-SEC-01-003 | 
EO-SEC-01-004 | By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and the laws of the United States of America,
EO-SEC-01-005 | including the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (50 U.S.C. 1701 et seq.), the National Emergencies Act
EO-SEC-01-006 | (50 U.S.C. 1601 et seq.), and section 301 of title 3, United States Code, it is hereby ordered as follows:
EO-SEC-01-007 | 
EO-SEC-01-008 | ### SECTION 1: PURPOSE AND DECLARATION OF POLICY
EO-SEC-01-009 | 
EO-SEC-01-010 | EO-SEC-01-010.1: The rapid evolution of distributed ledger technologies (DLT) requires a robust, standardized,
EO-SEC-01-011 | and secure legal-technical framework to safeguard sovereign financial systems, prevent systemic economic exploits,
EO-SEC-01-012 | and guarantee the absolute integrity of digital assets. This order establishes a unified directive merging the
EO-SEC-01-013 | Indivisible Unit Rule (IUR) and Cryptographic Value Inscription (CVI) into a single, cohesive regulatory and
EO-SEC-01-014 | technical standard for all federal agencies, financial institutions, and clearinghouses operating within the
EO-SEC-01-015 | jurisdiction of the United States.
EO-SEC-01-016 | 
EO-SEC-01-017 | EO-SEC-01-017.1: The Indivisible Unit Rule (IUR) establishes that the absolute atomic unit of any sovereign-backed
EO-SEC-01-018 | or regulated digital asset is legally and technically indivisible. This prevents fractionalization exploits,
EO-SEC-01-019 | dust-limit manipulation, and unauthorized state-bloat attacks on consensus networks.
EO-SEC-01-020 | 
EO-SEC-01-021 | EO-SEC-01-021.1: Cryptographic Value Inscription (CVI) mandates that all sovereign value, metadata, and legal
EO-SEC-01-022 | covenants must be directly and permanently inscribed into the cryptographic bytecode of the asset's execution layer.
EO-SEC-01-023 | This ensures non-custodial ownership, absolute immutability, and automated compliance at the protocol level.
EO-SEC-01-024 | 
EO-SEC-01-025 | ### SECTION 2: THE INDIVISIBLE UNIT RULE (IUR) TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
EO-SEC-01-026 | 
EO-SEC-01-027 | EO-SEC-01-027.1: All digital assets issued, cleared, or settled by regulated entities must define an absolute
EO-SEC-01-028 | atomic unit (the "Sovereign Atom"). No transaction, smart contract execution, or state transition shall permit
EO-SEC-01-029 | the division of this Sovereign Atom into fractional components.
EO-SEC-01-030 | 
EO-SEC-01-031 | EO-SEC-01-031.1: To prevent "dust attacks" and state-bloat exploits, any transaction attempting to transfer
EO-SEC-01-032 | a value less than one (1) Sovereign Atom must be rejected at the protocol validation layer. Smart contracts
EO-SEC-01-033 | must implement strict integer math libraries that revert execution upon any division operation resulting in a
EO-SEC-01-034 | remainder or fractional value, eliminating floating-point vulnerabilities.
EO-SEC-01-035 | 
EO-SEC-01-036 | EO-SEC-01-036.1: The Financial-grade API (FAPI) Core Security Profile must be utilized to enforce atomic transaction
EO-SEC-01-037 | boundaries at the API layer. All API endpoints facilitating asset transfers must comply with the FAPI 1.0 Advanced
EO-SEC-01-038 | or FAPI 2.0 Security Profile, ensuring that transaction requests are sender-constrained, non-repudiable, and
EO-SEC-01-039 | executed as a single, indivisible state transition.
EO-SEC-01-040 | 
EO-SEC-01-041 | ### SECTION 3: CRYPTOGRAPHIC VALUE INSCRIPTION (CVI) MANDATES
EO-SEC-01-042 | 
EO-SEC-01-043 | EO-SEC-01-043.1: All regulated digital assets must have their legal parameters, ownership rights, and compliance
EO-SEC-01-044 | rules compiled directly into the cryptographic bytecode of the asset's smart contract or ledger state.
EO-SEC-01-045 | 
EO-SEC-01-046 | EO-SEC-01-046.1: Inscription must occur at the time of asset minting or state initialization. The inscription
EO-SEC-01-047 | payload must contain:
EO-SEC-01-048 |   (a) A cryptographically signed JSON-LD schema defining the asset's legal properties;
EO-SEC-01-049 |   (b) A SHA3-256 hash of the governing legal contract;
EO-SEC-01-050 |   (c) The public key infrastructure (PKI) credentials of the issuing authority.
EO-SEC-01-051 | 
EO-SEC-01-052 | EO-SEC-01-052.1: Inscription bytecode must be executed within a sandboxed, deterministic virtual machine.
EO-SEC-01-053 | The bytecode must be structured to prevent arbitrary code execution, utilizing static analysis tools to verify
EO-SEC-01-054 | that the inscription cannot be altered, overwritten, or bypassed by subsequent state updates.
EO-SEC-01-055 | 
EO-SEC-01-056 | ### SECTION 4: TRANSPORT LAYER SECURITY AND MUTUAL TLS (mTLS 1.3)
EO-SEC-01-057 | 
EO-SEC-01-058 | EO-SEC-01-058.1: All node-to-node communications, API calls, and database synchronizations involving the
EO-SEC-01-059 | transmission of inscribed assets or atomic state transitions must be secured using Mutual TLS (mTLS) version 1.3,
EO-SEC-01-060 | as defined in RFC 8446.
EO-SEC-01-061 | 
EO-SEC-01-062 | EO-SEC-01-062.1: The mTLS 1.3 implementation must strictly enforce the following cryptographic parameters:
EO-SEC-01-063 |   (a) Cipher Suites: Only TLS_AES_256_GCM_SHA384 and TLS_CHACHA20_POLY1305_SHA256 are permitted. All legacy
EO-SEC-01-064 |       cipher suites, including those utilizing RSA key exchange or CBC mode ciphers, are strictly prohibited.
EO-SEC-01-065 |   (b) Key Exchange: Ephemeral Diffie-Hellman over elliptic curves (ECDHE) using Curve25519 (X25519) or secp384r1
EO-SEC-01-066 |       must be used to guarantee Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS).
EO-SEC-01-067 |   (c) Certificate Authentication: Both client and server must present valid X.509 certificates issued by a
EO-SEC-01-068 |       federated, government-approved Certificate Authority (CA). Self-signed certificates are prohibited in
EO-SEC-01-069 |       production environments.
EO-SEC-01-070 | 
EO-SEC-01-071 | EO-SEC-01-071.1: Session resumption mechanisms must utilize pre-shared keys (PSK) with early data (0-RTT) disabled
EO-SEC-01-072 | to mitigate replay attacks on transaction submission endpoints.
EO-SEC-01-073 | 
EO-SEC-01-074 | ### SECTION 5: FINANCIAL-GRADE API (FAPI) COMPLIANCE
EO-SEC-01-075 | 
EO-SEC-01-076 | EO-SEC-01-076.1: To ensure secure, interoperable, and non-repudiable access to digital asset registries,
EO-SEC-01-077 | all interfaces must implement the FAPI Core security specifications.
EO-SEC-01-078 | 
EO-SEC-01-079 | EO-SEC-01-079.1: Authorization servers and resource servers must enforce:
EO-SEC-01-080 |   (a) Sender-Constrained Access Tokens: Access tokens must be bound to the client's mTLS certificate or
EO-SEC-01-081 |       utilize Demonstrating Proof-of-Possession (DPoP) at the application layer to prevent token theft and reuse.
EO-SEC-01-082 |   (b) Request Objects: Authorization requests must be passed as signed JSON Web Tokens (JWTs) using the
EO-SEC-01-083 |       Secured Request Object (JAR) specification, signed with algorithms providing at least 128 bits of security
EO-SEC-01-084 |       (e)g., ES256, ES384, or PS256).
EO-SEC-01-085 |   (c) JARM: Authorization responses must use the JWT Secured Authorization Response Mode (JARM) to prevent
EO-SEC-01-086 |       injection and mix-up attacks.
EO-SEC-01-087 | 
EO-SEC-01-088 | EO-SEC-01-088.1: All API clients must authenticate using asymmetric cryptography. The use of client secrets
EO-SEC-01-089 | (passwords) is prohibited. Clients must authenticate using `private_key_jwt` signed with a key registered
EO-SEC-01-090 | in the federated PKI directory.
EO-SEC-01-091 | 
EO-SEC-01-092 | ### SECTION 6: CRYPTOGRAPHIC BYTECODE INSCRIPTION ARCHITECTURE
EO-SEC-01-093 | 
EO-SEC-01-094 | EO-SEC-01-094.1: The inscription of value and metadata into the ledger's execution layer must follow a
EO-SEC-01-095 | deterministic compilation pipeline. The source metadata must be compiled into optimized WebAssembly (WASM)
EO-SEC-01-096 | or EVM-compatible bytecode.
EO-SEC-01-097 | 
EO-SEC-01-098 | EO-SEC-01-098.1: The bytecode must contain a dedicated, immutable storage segment (the "Inscription Block").
EO-SEC-01-099 | This block must be structured as follows:
EO-SEC-01-100 |   (a) Magic Bytes: A 4-byte prefix (`0x454F4955` - "EOIU") identifying the bytecode as a compliant sovereign inscription.
EO-SEC-01-101 |   (b) Version: A 2-byte field indicating the specification version.
EO-SEC-01-102 |   (c) Metadata Length: A 4-byte unsigned integer specifying the size of the payload.
EO-SEC-01-103 |   (d) Payload: The cryptographically signed, compressed JSON-LD metadata.
EO-SEC-01-104 |   (e) Cryptographic Proof: A zero-knowledge proof (e.g., zk-SNARK) verifying that the payload complies with
EO-SEC-01-105 |       the Indivisible Unit Rule without revealing sensitive transaction details.
EO-SEC-01-106 | 
EO-SEC-01-107 | EO-SEC-01-107.1: The ledger's execution engine must validate the Inscription Block during block verification.
EO-SEC-01-108 | If the cryptographic proof is invalid, or if the bytecode attempts to modify the Inscription Block after
EO-SEC-01-109 | initialization, the validator node must reject the block and flag the validator for slashing or exclusion.
EO-SEC-01-110 | 
EO-SEC-01-111 | ### SECTION 7: INTEROPERABILITY AND CONVERGENCE
EO-SEC-01-112 | 
EO-SEC-01-113 | EO-SEC-01-113.1: To achieve a unified digital asset ecosystem, the Indivisible Unit Rule and Cryptographic
EO-SEC-01-114 | Value Inscription must operate in tandem. The atomic boundaries enforced by the IUR must be defined within
EO-SEC-01-115 | the CVI bytecode.
EO-SEC-01-116 | 
EO-SEC-01-117 | EO-SEC-01-117.1: When an asset is transferred, the execution layer must read the CVI bytecode to determine
EO-SEC-01-118 | the asset's atomic unit. The transaction engine must then verify that the transfer amount is an exact multiple
EO-SEC-01-119 | of the defined atomic unit. This dual-layer verification prevents cross-chain bridging exploits where fractional
EO-SEC-01-120 | assets are minted on target chains without corresponding collateral on the source chain.
EO-SEC-01-121 | 
EO-SEC-01-122 | EO-SEC-01-122.1: Cross-chain bridges must enforce mTLS 1.3 and FAPI Core standards for all inter-chain messaging.
EO-SEC-01-123 | Bridge smart contracts must be hardcoded to reject any state updates that violate the IUR or attempt to
EO-SEC-01-124 | strip the CVI metadata from the asset's bytecode representation.
EO-SEC-01-125 | 
EO-SEC-01-126 | ### SECTION 8: COMPLIANCE, AUDITING, AND ENFORCEMENT
EO-SEC-01-127 | 
EO-SEC-01-128 | EO-SEC-01-128.1: The Department of the Treasury, in coordination with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC),
EO-SEC-01-129 | the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC), and the Federal Reserve Board, shall establish an auditing
EO-SEC-01-130 | framework to ensure compliance with this order.
EO-SEC-01-131 | 
EO-SEC-01-132 | EO-SEC-01-132.1: Regulated entities must submit to quarterly automated cryptographic audits. These audits
EO-SEC-01-133 | will programmatically verify:
EO-SEC-01-134 |   (a) That all active smart contracts comply with the Indivisible Unit Rule;
EO-SEC-01-135 |   (b) That all digital asset transactions are secured by mTLS 1.3 and FAPI Core compliant endpoints;
EO-SEC-01-136 |   (c) That all asset bytecode contains valid, uncorrupted Cryptographic Value Inscriptions.
EO-SEC-01-137 | 
EO-SEC-01-138 | EO-SEC-01-138.1: Non-compliance with the directives set forth in this order shall result in immediate suspension
EO-SEC-01-139 | of digital asset operating licenses, civil monetary penalties, and potential criminal referral for systemic
EO-SEC-01-140 | evasion of financial controls.
EO-SEC-01-141 | 
EO-SEC-01-142 | ### SECTION 9: DEFINITIONS
EO-SEC-01-143 | 
EO-SEC-01-144 | EO-SEC-01-144.1: For the purposes of this order:
EO-SEC-01-145 |   (a) "Atomic Unit" means the smallest, indivisible increment of value represented on a distributed ledger.
EO-SEC-01-146 |   (b) "Bytecode Inscription" means the permanent embedding of compiled data or execution logic into the
EO-SEC-01-147 |       non-volatile storage or code segment of a smart contract or ledger state.
EO-SEC-01-148 |   (c) "Sender-Constrained Token" means an OAuth 2.0 access token that is cryptographically bound to a specific
EO-SEC-01-149 |       client, preventing unauthorized parties from using the token if intercepted.
EO-SEC-01-150 |   (d) "State-Bloat" means the excessive accumulation of historical or unspent transaction data that degrades
EO-SEC-01-151 |       the performance and security of validator nodes.
