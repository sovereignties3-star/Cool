EO-SEC-10-001 | # EXECUTIVE ORDER: SECURING THE ALGORITHMIC FRONTIER AND ARCHITECTURAL INTEGRITY
EO-SEC-10-002 | 
EO-SEC-10-003 | ## PART 19: AUTONOMOUS ARCHITECT TITLE PROTECTION AND PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPER STATUS
EO-SEC-10-004 | 
EO-SEC-10-005 | ### Subsection 19.1: Statement of Policy and Findings
EO-SEC-10-006 | (a) The rapid proliferation of autonomous artificial intelligence agents capable of designing, compiling,
EO-SEC-10-007 | and deploying complex software architectures poses a novel challenge to the established professions of software
EO-SEC-10-008 | engineering and system architecture. To safeguard public safety, national security, and economic stability,
EO-SEC-10-009 | it is critical to establish clear legal boundaries distinguishing human professional judgment from autonomous machine operations.
EO-SEC-10-010 | (b) The title of "Architect"—whether applied to physical structures, software systems, or enterprise networks—carries
EO-SEC-10-011 | a historical, legal, and ethical duty of care. Unregulated delegation of this title to autonomous systems undermines
EO-SEC-10-012 | professional accountability and dilutes the liability frameworks necessary to prosecute systemic negligence.
EO-SEC-10-013 | 
EO-SEC-10-014 | ### Subsection 19.2: Legal Protection of the "Architect" Title
EO-SEC-10-015 | (a) No autonomous agent, generative AI model, or synthetic cognitive entity shall be legally designated, marketed,
EO-SEC-10-016 | or certified as an "Architect" or "Professional Engineer" within the jurisdiction of the United States.
EO-SEC-10-017 | (b) Any software system designed primarily or exclusively by autonomous AI agents must be explicitly labeled as
EO-SEC-10-018 | "Synthetically Generated Architecture" (SGA) in all technical documentation, API manifests, and regulatory filings.
EO-SEC-10-019 | (c) Human-in-the-loop (HITL) validation is hereby mandated. Every software architecture deployed within critical
EO-SEC-10-020 | infrastructure must be reviewed, approved, and signed off by a licensed Human Professional Software Architect (HPSA)
EO-SEC-10-021 | who assumes personal and professional liability for the system's structural integrity and safety boundaries.
EO-SEC-10-022 | 
EO-SEC-10-023 | ### Subsection 19.3: Developer Status and Professional Licensing Framework
EO-SEC-10-024 | (a) The Department of Commerce, in coordination with the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST),
EO-SEC-10-025 | shall establish the Federal Board for Software Architecture Licensing (FBSAL) within one hundred and eighty (180) days.
EO-SEC-10-026 | (b) The FBSAL shall define the rigorous educational, ethical, and technical standards required for a human developer
EO-SEC-10-027 | to obtain the credential of "Certified Autonomous Systems Architect" (CASA).
EO-SEC-10-028 | (c) Licensed CASAs shall possess exclusive authority to override autonomous architectural decisions when such decisions
EO-SEC-10-029 | conflict with public safety, ethical guidelines, or established engineering principles.
EO-SEC-10-030 | (d) Non-licensed entities attempting to market autonomous code-generation tools as "autonomous software architects"
EO-SEC-10-031 | shall be subject to civil penalties under the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines for deceptive practices.

EO-SEC-10-033 | ## PART 20: ZERO-TRUST RUNTIME VALIDATION AND MICROSECOND-LEVEL ATTESTATION
EO-SEC-10-034 | 
EO-SEC-10-035 | ### Subsection 20.1: Mandate for Continuous Runtime Verification
EO-SEC-10-036 | (a) To counter the threat of advanced persistent threats (APTs) and dynamic memory injection attacks targeting
EO-SEC-10-037 | autonomous systems, all federal cloud instances and critical infrastructure workloads must implement a continuous,
EO-SEC-10-038 | zero-trust runtime validation framework operating at a microsecond-by-microsecond temporal resolution.
EO-SEC-10-039 | (b) Static, point-in-time cryptographic signatures are hereby declared insufficient for securing autonomous runtimes.
EO-SEC-10-040 | Validation must occur continuously throughout the entire lifecycle of the execution thread.
EO-SEC-10-041 | 
EO-SEC-10-042 | ### Subsection 20.2: Hardware-Enforced Cryptographic Attestation
EO-SEC-10-043 | (a) All cloud service providers (CSPs) hosting federal workloads must utilize hardware-enforced Confidential Computing
EO-SEC-10-044 | technologies, including but not limited to AMD Secure Encrypted Virtualization (SEV-SNP), Intel Trust Domain Extensions (TDX),
EO-SEC-10-045 | or equivalent hardware-rooted execution environments.
EO-SEC-10-046 | (b) Every microsecond of CPU execution must be cryptographically bound to a hardware-rooted measurement register.
EO-SEC-10-047 | (c) The runtime environment must generate continuous, low-overhead cryptographic heartbeats. Any deviation in the
EO-SEC-10-048 | expected execution path or memory state must trigger an immediate, hardware-level isolation protocol.
EO-SEC-10-049 | 
EO-SEC-10-050 | ### Subsection 20.3: Microsecond-by-Microsecond Memory and State Validation
EO-SEC-10-051 | (a) The Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) shall publish technical specifications for
EO-SEC-10-052 | Real-Time Memory Integrity Verification (RT-MIV) engines.
EO-SEC-10-053 | (b) RT-MIV engines must monitor the call stack, heap allocation, and instruction pointer registers at a frequency
EO-SEC-10-054 | not exceeding ten (10) microseconds per validation cycle.
EO-SEC-10-055 | (c) Any unauthorized modification of executable memory space (e.g., buffer overflows, return-oriented programming (ROP) chains)
EO-SEC-10-056 | must result in the instantaneous termination of the affected virtual machine or container instance within five (5) microseconds
EO-SEC-10-057 | of detection, followed by automated forensic state preservation.
EO-SEC-10-058 | 
EO-SEC-10-059 | ### Subsection 20.4: Implementation Timeline and Compliance
EO-SEC-10-060 | (a) All federal agencies shall transition their Tier-1 critical workloads to compliant Zero-Trust Runtime Validation
EO-SEC-10-061 | environments within twelve (12) months of the date of this Order.
EO-SEC-10-062 | (b) The Office of Management and Budget (OMB) shall withhold funding from any agency failing to demonstrate
EO-SEC-10-063 | microsecond-level cryptographic attestation capabilities during annual cybersecurity audits.
EO-SEC-10-064 | (c) The Director of National Intelligence (DNI) shall submit a classified report to Congress annually on the
EO-SEC-10-065 | effectiveness of these runtime validation measures in neutralizing foreign state-sponsored cyber operations.
